# PA.IV.J — Glassy Water Approach and Landing (ASES, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=38)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASES, AMES

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with glassy water approach and landing.

> [!note] Note
> If a glassy water condition does not exist, the applicant must be evaluated by simulating the Task.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.J.K1** A stabilized approach, including energy management concepts. ^pa-iv-j-k1

- **PA.IV.J.K2** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on approach and landing performance. ^pa-iv-j-k2

- **PA.IV.J.K3** When and why glassy water techniques are used. ^pa-iv-j-k3

- **PA.IV.J.K4** How a glassy water approach and landing is executed. ^pa-iv-j-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.J.R1** Selection of approach path and touchdown area based on pilot capability, airplane performance and limitations, and available distance. ^pa-iv-j-r1

- **PA.IV.J.R2** Water surface/condition. ^pa-iv-j-r2

- **PA.IV.J.R3** Planning for a go-around and rejected landing. ^pa-iv-j-r3

- **PA.IV.J.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-j-r4

- **PA.IV.J.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-j-r5

- **PA.IV.J.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-j-r6

- **PA.IV.J.R7** Gear position in an amphibious airplane. ^pa-iv-j-r7

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.J.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-j-s1

- **PA.IV.J.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-j-s2

- **PA.IV.J.S3** Scan the landing area for traffic and obstructions. ^pa-iv-j-s3

- **PA.IV.J.S4** Select a proper approach and landing path considering the landing surface, visual attitude references, water depth, and collision hazards. ^pa-iv-j-s4

- **PA.IV.J.S5** Establish the recommended approach and landing configuration, airspeed, and trim, and adjust pitch attitude and power as required to maintain a stabilized approach. ^pa-iv-j-s5

- **PA.IV.J.S6** Maintain manufacturer’s published approach airspeed or in its absence not more than 1.3 VSO, +10/-5 knots. ^pa-iv-j-s6

- **PA.IV.J.S7** Make smooth, timely, and correct power and control adjustments to maintain proper pitch attitude and rate of descent to touchdown. ^pa-iv-j-s7

- **PA.IV.J.S8** Contact the water in a proper pitch attitude, and slow to idle taxi speed. ^pa-iv-j-s8

- **PA.IV.J.S9** Maintain directional control throughout the approach and landing. ^pa-iv-j-s9
