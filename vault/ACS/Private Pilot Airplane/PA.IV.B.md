# PA.IV.B — Normal Approach and Landing

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=27)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with normal approach and landing with emphasis on proper use and coordination of flight controls.

> [!note] Note
> If a crosswind condition does not exist, the applicant’s knowledge of crosswind elements must be evaluated through oral testing.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.B.K1** A stabilized approach, including energy management concepts. ^pa-iv-b-k1

- **PA.IV.B.K2** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on approach and landing performance. ^pa-iv-b-k2

- **PA.IV.B.K3** Wind correction techniques on approach and landing. ^pa-iv-b-k3

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.B.R1** Selection of runway/landing surface, approach path, and touchdown area based on pilot capability, aircraft performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-b-r1

- **PA.IV.B.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-b-r2

    - **PA.IV.B.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-b-r2a

    - **PA.IV.B.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-b-r2b

    - **PA.IV.B.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-b-r2c

    - **PA.IV.B.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-b-r2d

    - **PA.IV.B.R2e** e. Landing surface/condition ^pa-iv-b-r2e

- **PA.IV.B.R3** Planning for: ^pa-iv-b-r3

    - **PA.IV.B.R3a** a. Rejected landing and go-around ^pa-iv-b-r3a

    - **PA.IV.B.R3b** b. Land and hold short operations (LAHSO) ^pa-iv-b-r3b

- **PA.IV.B.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-b-r4

- **PA.IV.B.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-b-r5

- **PA.IV.B.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-b-r6

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.B.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-b-s1

- **PA.IV.B.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-b-s2

- **PA.IV.B.S3** Ensure the airplane is aligned with the correct/assigned runway or landing surface. ^pa-iv-b-s3

- **PA.IV.B.S4** Scan the runway or landing surface and adjoining area for traffic and obstructions. ^pa-iv-b-s4

- **PA.IV.B.S5** Select and aim for a suitable touchdown point considering the wind conditions, landing surface, and obstructions. ^pa-iv-b-s5

- **PA.IV.B.S6** Establish the recommended approach and landing configuration, airspeed, and trim, and adjust pitch attitude and power as required to maintain a stabilized approach. ^pa-iv-b-s6

- **PA.IV.B.S7** Maintain manufacturer’s published approach airspeed or in its absence not more than 1.3 times the stalling speed or the minimum steady flight speed in the landing configuration (VSO), +10/-5 knots with gust factor applied. ^pa-iv-b-s7

- **PA.IV.B.S8** Maintain directional control and appropriate crosswind correction throughout the approach and landing. ^pa-iv-b-s8

- **PA.IV.B.S9** Make smooth, timely, and correct control application during round out and touchdown. ^pa-iv-b-s9

- **PA.IV.B.S10** Touch down at a proper pitch attitude, within 400 feet beyond or on the specified point, with no side drift, and with the airplane’s longitudinal axis aligned with and over the runway center/landing path. ^pa-iv-b-s10

- **PA.IV.B.S11** Execute a timely go-around if the approach cannot be made within the tolerances specified above or for any other condition that may result in an unsafe approach or landing. ^pa-iv-b-s11

- **PA.IV.B.S12** Use runway incursion avoidance procedures, if applicable. ^pa-iv-b-s12
