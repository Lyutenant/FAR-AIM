# PA.VI.D — Lost Procedures

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=48)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.VI|Area of Operation VI — Navigation]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; VFR Navigation Charts

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with lost procedures and can take appropriate steps to achieve a satisfactory outcome if lost.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.VI.D.K1** Methods to determine position. ^pa-vi-d-k1

- **PA.VI.D.K2** Assistance available if lost (e.g., radar services, communication procedures). ^pa-vi-d-k2

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.VI.D.R1** Collision hazards. ^pa-vi-d-r1

- **PA.VI.D.R2** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-vi-d-r2

- **PA.VI.D.R3** Recording times over waypoints. ^pa-vi-d-r3

- **PA.VI.D.R4** When to seek assistance or declare an emergency in a deteriorating situation. ^pa-vi-d-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.VI.D.S1** Use an appropriate method to determine position. ^pa-vi-d-s1

- **PA.VI.D.S2** Maintain an appropriate heading and climb as necessary. ^pa-vi-d-s2

- **PA.VI.D.S3** Identify prominent landmarks. ^pa-vi-d-s3

- **PA.VI.D.S4** Use navigation systems/facilities or contact an ATC facility for assistance. ^pa-vi-d-s4

- **PA.VI.D.S5** Select an appropriate course of action. ^pa-vi-d-s5
