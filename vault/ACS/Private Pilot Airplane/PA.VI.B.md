# PA.VI.B — Navigation Systems and Radar Services

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=47)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.VI|Area of Operation VI — Navigation]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AC 91-78; AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with navigation systems and radar services.

> [!note] Note
> The evaluator should reference the manufacturer’s equipment supplement(s) as necessary for appropriate limitations, procedures, etc.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.VI.B.K1** Ground-based navigation (identification, orientation, course determination, equipment, tests, regulations, interference, appropriate use of navigation data, and signal integrity). ^pa-vi-b-k1

- **PA.VI.B.K2** Satellite-based navigation (e.g., equipment, regulations, authorized use of databases, and Receiver Autonomous Integrity Monitoring (RAIM)). ^pa-vi-b-k2

- **PA.VI.B.K3** Radar assistance to visual flight rules (VFR) aircraft (e.g., operations, equipment, available services, traffic advisories). ^pa-vi-b-k3

- **PA.VI.B.K4** Transponder (Mode(s) A, C, and S) and Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast (ADS-B). ^pa-vi-b-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.VI.B.R1** Management of automated navigation and autoflight systems. ^pa-vi-b-r1

- **PA.VI.B.R2** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-vi-b-r2

- **PA.VI.B.R3** Limitations of the navigation system in use. ^pa-vi-b-r3

- **PA.VI.B.R4** Loss of a navigation signal. ^pa-vi-b-r4

- **PA.VI.B.R5** Use of an electronic flight bag (EFB), if used. ^pa-vi-b-r5

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.VI.B.S1** Use an airborne electronic navigation system. ^pa-vi-b-s1

- **PA.VI.B.S2** Determine the airplane’s position using the navigation system. ^pa-vi-b-s2

- **PA.VI.B.S3** Intercept and track a given course, radial, or bearing. ^pa-vi-b-s3

- **PA.VI.B.S4** Recognize and describe the indication of station or waypoint passage. ^pa-vi-b-s4

- **PA.VI.B.S5** Recognize signal loss or interference and take appropriate action, if applicable. ^pa-vi-b-s5

- **PA.VI.B.S6** Use proper communication procedures when utilizing radar services. ^pa-vi-b-s6

- **PA.VI.B.S7** Maintain the selected altitude, ±200 feet and heading, ±15°. ^pa-vi-b-s7
