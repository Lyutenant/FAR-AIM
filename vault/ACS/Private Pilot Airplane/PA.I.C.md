# PA.I.C — Weather Information

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=11)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.I|Area of Operation I — Preflight Preparation]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with weather information for a flight under VFR.

> [!note] Note
> If K2 is selected, the evaluator must assess the applicant’s knowledge of at least three sub-elements.

> [!note] Note
> If K3 is selected, the evaluator must assess the applicant’s knowledge of at least three sub-elements.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.I.C.K1** Sources of weather data (e.g., National Weather Service, Flight Service) for flight planning purposes. ^pa-i-c-k1

- **PA.I.C.K2** Acceptable weather products and resources required for preflight planning, current and forecast weather for departure, en route, and arrival phases of flight such as: ^pa-i-c-k2

    - **PA.I.C.K2a** a. Airport Observations (METAR and SPECI) and Pilot Observations (PIREP) ^pa-i-c-k2a

    - **PA.I.C.K2b** b. Surface Analysis Chart, Ceiling and Visibility Chart (CVA) ^pa-i-c-k2b

    - **PA.I.C.K2c** c. Terminal Aerodrome Forecasts (TAF) ^pa-i-c-k2c

    - **PA.I.C.K2d** d. Graphical Forecasts for Aviation (GFA) ^pa-i-c-k2d

    - **PA.I.C.K2e** e. Wind and Temperature Aloft Forecast (FB) ^pa-i-c-k2e

    - **PA.I.C.K2f** f. Convective Outlook (AC) ^pa-i-c-k2f

    - **PA.I.C.K2g** g. Inflight Aviation Weather Advisories including Airmen's Meteorological Information (AIRMET), Significant Meteorological Information (SIGMET), and Convective SIGMET ^pa-i-c-k2g

- **PA.I.C.K3** Meteorology applicable to the departure, en route, alternate, and destination under visual flight rules (VFR) in Visual Meteorological Conditions (VMC), including expected climate and hazardous conditions such as: ^pa-i-c-k3

    - **PA.I.C.K3a** a. Atmospheric composition and stability ^pa-i-c-k3a

    - **PA.I.C.K3b** b. Wind (e.g., windshear, mountain wave, factors affecting wind, etc.) ^pa-i-c-k3b

    - **PA.I.C.K3c** c. Temperature and heat exchange ^pa-i-c-k3c

    - **PA.I.C.K3d** d. Moisture/precipitation ^pa-i-c-k3d

    - **PA.I.C.K3e** e. Weather system formation, including air masses and fronts ^pa-i-c-k3e

    - **PA.I.C.K3f** f. Clouds ^pa-i-c-k3f

    - **PA.I.C.K3g** g. Turbulence ^pa-i-c-k3g

    - **PA.I.C.K3h** h. Thunderstorms and microbursts ^pa-i-c-k3h

    - **PA.I.C.K3i** i. Icing and freezing level information ^pa-i-c-k3i

    - **PA.I.C.K3j** j. Fog/mist ^pa-i-c-k3j

    - **PA.I.C.K3k** k. Frost ^pa-i-c-k3k

    - **PA.I.C.K3l** l. Obstructions to visibility (e.g., smoke, haze, volcanic ash, etc.) ^pa-i-c-k3l

- **PA.I.C.K4** Flight deck instrument displays of digital weather and aeronautical information. ^pa-i-c-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.I.C.R1** Making the go/no-go and continue/divert decisions, including: ^pa-i-c-r1

    - **PA.I.C.R1a** a. Circumstances that would make diversion prudent ^pa-i-c-r1a

    - **PA.I.C.R1b** b. Personal weather minimums ^pa-i-c-r1b

    - **PA.I.C.R1c** c. Hazardous weather conditions, including known or forecast icing or turbulence aloft ^pa-i-c-r1c

- **PA.I.C.R2** Use and limitations of: ^pa-i-c-r2

    - **PA.I.C.R2a** a. Installed onboard weather equipment ^pa-i-c-r2a

    - **PA.I.C.R2b** b. Aviation weather reports and forecasts ^pa-i-c-r2b

    - **PA.I.C.R2c** c. Inflight weather resources ^pa-i-c-r2c

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.I.C.S1** Use available aviation weather resources to obtain an adequate weather briefing. ^pa-i-c-s1

- **PA.I.C.S2** Analyze the implications of at least three of the conditions listed in K3a through K3l, using actual weather or weather conditions provided by the evaluator. ^pa-i-c-s2

- **PA.I.C.S3** Correlate weather information to make a go/no-go decision. ^pa-i-c-s3
