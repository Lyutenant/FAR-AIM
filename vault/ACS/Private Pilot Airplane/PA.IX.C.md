# PA.IX.C — Systems and Equipment Malfunctions

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=60)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IX|Area of Operation IX — Emergency Operations]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with system and equipment malfunctions appropriate to the airplane provided for the practical test.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IX.C.K1** Causes of partial or complete power loss related to the specific type of powerplant(s). ^pa-ix-c-k1

    - **PA.IX.C.K1a** a. \[Archived] ^pa-ix-c-k1a

    - **PA.IX.C.K1b** b. \[Archived] ^pa-ix-c-k1b

    - **PA.IX.C.K1c** c. \[Archived] ^pa-ix-c-k1c

    - **PA.IX.C.K1d** d. \[Archived] ^pa-ix-c-k1d

- **PA.IX.C.K2** System and equipment malfunctions specific to the aircraft, including: ^pa-ix-c-k2

    - **PA.IX.C.K2a** a. Electrical malfunction ^pa-ix-c-k2a

    - **PA.IX.C.K2b** b. Vacuum/pressure and associated flight instrument malfunctions ^pa-ix-c-k2b

    - **PA.IX.C.K2c** c. Pitot-static system malfunction ^pa-ix-c-k2c

    - **PA.IX.C.K2d** d. Electronic flight deck display malfunction ^pa-ix-c-k2d

    - **PA.IX.C.K2e** e. Landing gear or flap malfunction ^pa-ix-c-k2e

    - **PA.IX.C.K2f** f. Inoperative trim ^pa-ix-c-k2f

- **PA.IX.C.K3** Causes and remedies for smoke or fire onboard the aircraft. ^pa-ix-c-k3

- **PA.IX.C.K4** Any other system specific to the aircraft (e.g., supplemental oxygen, deicing). ^pa-ix-c-k4

- **PA.IX.C.K5** Inadvertent door or window opening. ^pa-ix-c-k5

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IX.C.R1** Checklist usage for a system or equipment malfunction. ^pa-ix-c-r1

- **PA.IX.C.R2** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-ix-c-r2

- **PA.IX.C.R3** Undesired aircraft state. ^pa-ix-c-r3

- **PA.IX.C.R4** Startle response. ^pa-ix-c-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IX.C.S1** Determine appropriate action for simulated emergencies specified by the evaluator, from at least three of the elements or sub-elements listed in K1 through K5. ^pa-ix-c-s1

- **PA.IX.C.S2** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-ix-c-s2
