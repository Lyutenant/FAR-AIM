# PA.I.H — Human Factors

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=16)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.I|Area of Operation I — Preflight Preparation]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with personal health, flight physiology, and aeromedical and human factors related to safety of flight.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.I.H.K1** Symptoms, recognition, causes, effects, and corrective actions associated with aeromedical and physiological issues, including: ^pa-i-h-k1

    - **PA.I.H.K1a** a. Hypoxia ^pa-i-h-k1a

    - **PA.I.H.K1b** b. Hyperventilation ^pa-i-h-k1b

    - **PA.I.H.K1c** c. Middle ear and sinus problems ^pa-i-h-k1c

    - **PA.I.H.K1d** d. Spatial disorientation ^pa-i-h-k1d

    - **PA.I.H.K1e** e. Motion sickness ^pa-i-h-k1e

    - **PA.I.H.K1f** f. Carbon monoxide poisoning ^pa-i-h-k1f

    - **PA.I.H.K1g** g. Stress ^pa-i-h-k1g

    - **PA.I.H.K1h** h. Fatigue ^pa-i-h-k1h

    - **PA.I.H.K1i** i. Dehydration and nutrition ^pa-i-h-k1i

    - **PA.I.H.K1j** j. Hypothermia ^pa-i-h-k1j

    - **PA.I.H.K1k** k. Optical illusions ^pa-i-h-k1k

    - **PA.I.H.K1l** l. Dissolved nitrogen in the bloodstream after scuba dives ^pa-i-h-k1l

- **PA.I.H.K2** Regulations regarding use of alcohol and drugs. ^pa-i-h-k2

- **PA.I.H.K3** Effects of alcohol, drugs, and over-the-counter medications. ^pa-i-h-k3

- **PA.I.H.K4** Aeronautical Decision-Making (ADM) to include using Crew Resource Management (CRM) or Single-Pilot Resource Management (SRM), as appropriate. ^pa-i-h-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.I.H.R1** Aeromedical and physiological issues. ^pa-i-h-r1

- **PA.I.H.R2** Hazardous attitudes. ^pa-i-h-r2

- **PA.I.H.R3** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-i-h-r3

- **PA.I.H.R4** Confirmation and expectation bias. ^pa-i-h-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.I.H.S1** Associate the symptoms and effects for at least three of the conditions listed in K1a through K1l with the cause(s) and corrective action(s). ^pa-i-h-s1

- **PA.I.H.S2** Perform self-assessment, including fitness for flight and personal minimums, for actual flight or a scenario given by the evaluator. ^pa-i-h-s2
