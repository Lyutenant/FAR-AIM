# PA.IV.M — Forward Slip to a Landing (ASEL, ASES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=41)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASEL, ASES

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with forward slip to a landing.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.M.K1** Concepts of energy management during a forward slip approach. ^pa-iv-m-k1

- **PA.IV.M.K2** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on approach and landing performance. ^pa-iv-m-k2

- **PA.IV.M.K3** Wind correction techniques during forward slip. ^pa-iv-m-k3

- **PA.IV.M.K4** When and why a forward slip approach is used during an approach. ^pa-iv-m-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.M.R1** Selection of runway/landing surface, approach path, and touchdown area based on pilot capability, aircraft performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-m-r1

- **PA.IV.M.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-m-r2

    - **PA.IV.M.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-m-r2a

    - **PA.IV.M.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-m-r2b

    - **PA.IV.M.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-m-r2c

    - **PA.IV.M.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-m-r2d

    - **PA.IV.M.R2e** e. Landing surface/condition ^pa-iv-m-r2e

- **PA.IV.M.R3** Planning for: ^pa-iv-m-r3

    - **PA.IV.M.R3a** a. Rejected landing and go-around ^pa-iv-m-r3a

    - **PA.IV.M.R3b** b. Land and hold short operations (LAHSO) ^pa-iv-m-r3b

- **PA.IV.M.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-m-r4

- **PA.IV.M.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-m-r5

- **PA.IV.M.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-m-r6

- **PA.IV.M.R7** Forward slip operations, including fuel flowage, tail stalls with flaps, and airspeed control. ^pa-iv-m-r7

- **PA.IV.M.R8** Surface contact with the airplane’s longitudinal axis misaligned. ^pa-iv-m-r8

- **PA.IV.M.R9** Unstable approach. ^pa-iv-m-r9

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.M.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-m-s1

- **PA.IV.M.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-m-s2

- **PA.IV.M.S3** Plan and follow a flightpath to the selected landing area considering altitude, wind, terrain, and obstructions. ^pa-iv-m-s3

- **PA.IV.M.S4** Select the most suitable touchdown point based on wind, landing surface, obstructions, and airplane limitations. ^pa-iv-m-s4

- **PA.IV.M.S5** Position airplane on downwind leg, parallel to landing runway or selected landing surface. ^pa-iv-m-s5

- **PA.IV.M.S6** Configure the airplane correctly. ^pa-iv-m-s6

- **PA.IV.M.S7** As necessary, correlate crosswind with direction of forward slip and transition to side slip before touchdown. ^pa-iv-m-s7

- **PA.IV.M.S8** Touch down at a proper pitch attitude, within 400 feet beyond or on the specified point, with no side drift, and with the airplane’s longitudinal axis aligned with and over the runway center/landing path. ^pa-iv-m-s8

- **PA.IV.M.S9** Maintain a ground track aligned with the runway center/landing path. ^pa-iv-m-s9
