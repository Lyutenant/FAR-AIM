# PA.XII.A — After Landing, Parking, and Securing (ASEL, AMEL)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=72)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.XII|Area of Operation XII — Postflight Procedures]] · **Applies to:** ASEL, AMEL

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with after landing, parking, and securing procedures.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.XII.A.K1** Airplane shutdown, securing, and postflight inspection. ^pa-xii-a-k1

- **PA.XII.A.K2** Documenting in-flight/postflight discrepancies. ^pa-xii-a-k2

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.XII.A.R1** Activities and distractions. ^pa-xii-a-r1

- **PA.XII.A.R2** \[Archived] ^pa-xii-a-r2

- **PA.XII.A.R3** Airport specific security procedures. ^pa-xii-a-r3

- **PA.XII.A.R4** Disembarking passengers safely on the ramp and monitoring passenger movement while on the ramp. ^pa-xii-a-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.XII.A.S1** \[Archived] ^pa-xii-a-s1

- **PA.XII.A.S2** Park in an appropriate area, considering the safety of nearby persons and property. ^pa-xii-a-s2

- **PA.XII.A.S3** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-xii-a-s3

- **PA.XII.A.S4** Conduct a postflight inspection and document discrepancies and servicing requirements, if any. ^pa-xii-a-s4

- **PA.XII.A.S5** Secure the airplane. ^pa-xii-a-s5
