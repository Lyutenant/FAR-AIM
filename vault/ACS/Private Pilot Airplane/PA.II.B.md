# PA.II.B — Flight Deck Management

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=19)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.II|Area of Operation II — Preflight Procedures]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

14 CFR part 91; AC 120-71; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[Part 91]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with flight deck management practices.

> [!note] Note
> See Appendix 2: Safety of Flight.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.II.B.K1** Passenger briefing requirements, including operation and required use of safety restraint systems. ^pa-ii-b-k1

- **PA.II.B.K2** Use of appropriate checklists. ^pa-ii-b-k2

- **PA.II.B.K3** Requirements for current and appropriate navigation data. ^pa-ii-b-k3

- **PA.II.B.K4** Securing items and cargo. ^pa-ii-b-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.II.B.R1** Use of systems or equipment, including automation and portable electronic devices. ^pa-ii-b-r1

- **PA.II.B.R2** Inoperative equipment. ^pa-ii-b-r2

- **PA.II.B.R3** Passenger distractions. ^pa-ii-b-r3

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.II.B.S1** Secure all items in the aircraft. ^pa-ii-b-s1

- **PA.II.B.S2** Conduct an appropriate passenger briefing, including identifying the pilot-in-command (PIC), use of safety belts, shoulder harnesses, doors, passenger conduct, sterile aircraft, propeller blade avoidance, and emergency procedures. ^pa-ii-b-s2

- **PA.II.B.S3** Properly program and manage the aircraft’s automation, as applicable. ^pa-ii-b-s3

- **PA.II.B.S4** Appropriately manage risks by utilizing ADM, including SRM/CRM. ^pa-ii-b-s4
