# PA.IV.H — Confined Area Approach and Landing (ASES, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=35)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASES, AMES

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with confined area approach and landing.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.H.K1** A stabilized approach, including energy management concepts. ^pa-iv-h-k1

- **PA.IV.H.K2** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on approach and landing performance. ^pa-iv-h-k2

- **PA.IV.H.K3** Wind correction techniques on approach and landing. ^pa-iv-h-k3

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.H.R1** Selection of approach path and touchdown area based on pilot capability, airplane performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-h-r1

- **PA.IV.H.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-h-r2

    - **PA.IV.H.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-h-r2a

    - **PA.IV.H.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-h-r2b

    - **PA.IV.H.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-h-r2c

    - **PA.IV.H.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-h-r2d

    - **PA.IV.H.R2e** e. Water surface/condition ^pa-iv-h-r2e

- **PA.IV.H.R3** Planning for a go-around and rejected landing. ^pa-iv-h-r3

- **PA.IV.H.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-h-r4

- **PA.IV.H.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-h-r5

- **PA.IV.H.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-h-r6

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.H.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-h-s1

- **PA.IV.H.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-h-s2

- **PA.IV.H.S3** Ensure the airplane is aligned for an approach to the correct/assigned landing surface. ^pa-iv-h-s3

- **PA.IV.H.S4** Scan the landing area for traffic and obstructions. ^pa-iv-h-s4

- **PA.IV.H.S5** Select and aim for a suitable touchdown point considering the wind conditions, landing surface, and obstructions. ^pa-iv-h-s5

- **PA.IV.H.S6** Establish the recommended approach and landing configuration, airspeed, and trim, and adjust pitch attitude and power as required to maintain a stabilized approach. ^pa-iv-h-s6

- **PA.IV.H.S7** Maintain manufacturer’s published approach airspeed or in its absence not more than 1.3 VSO, +10/-5 knots with gust factor applied. ^pa-iv-h-s7

- **PA.IV.H.S8** Maintain directional control and appropriate crosswind correction throughout the approach and landing. ^pa-iv-h-s8

- **PA.IV.H.S9** Make smooth, timely, and correct control application before, during, and after touchdown. ^pa-iv-h-s9

- **PA.IV.H.S10** Contact the water at the recommended airspeed with a proper pitch attitude for the surface conditions. ^pa-iv-h-s10

- **PA.IV.H.S11** Touch down at a proper pitch attitude, within 200 feet beyond or on the specified point, with no side drift, minimum float, and with the airplane’s longitudinal axis aligned with the projected landing path. ^pa-iv-h-s11

- **PA.IV.H.S12** Execute a timely go-around if the approach cannot be made within the tolerances specified above or for any other condition that may result in an unsafe approach or landing. ^pa-iv-h-s12

- **PA.IV.H.S13** Apply elevator control as necessary to stop in the shortest distance consistent with safety. ^pa-iv-h-s13
