# PA.VI.A — Pilotage and Dead Reckoning

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=46)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.VI|Area of Operation VI — Navigation]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; VFR Navigation Charts

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with pilotage and dead reckoning.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.VI.A.K1** Pilotage and dead reckoning. ^pa-vi-a-k1

- **PA.VI.A.K2** Magnetic compass errors. ^pa-vi-a-k2

- **PA.VI.A.K3** Topography. ^pa-vi-a-k3

- **PA.VI.A.K4** Selection of appropriate: ^pa-vi-a-k4

    - **PA.VI.A.K4a** a. Route ^pa-vi-a-k4a

    - **PA.VI.A.K4b** b. Altitude(s) ^pa-vi-a-k4b

    - **PA.VI.A.K4c** c. Checkpoints ^pa-vi-a-k4c

- **PA.VI.A.K5** Plotting a course, including: ^pa-vi-a-k5

    - **PA.VI.A.K5a** a. Determining heading, speed, and course ^pa-vi-a-k5a

    - **PA.VI.A.K5b** b. Wind correction angle ^pa-vi-a-k5b

    - **PA.VI.A.K5c** c. Estimating time, speed, and distance ^pa-vi-a-k5c

    - **PA.VI.A.K5d** d. True airspeed and density altitude ^pa-vi-a-k5d

- **PA.VI.A.K6** Power setting selection. ^pa-vi-a-k6

- **PA.VI.A.K7** Planned calculations versus actual results and required corrections. ^pa-vi-a-k7

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.VI.A.R1** Collision hazards. ^pa-vi-a-r1

- **PA.VI.A.R2** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-vi-a-r2

- **PA.VI.A.R3** Unplanned fuel/power consumption, if applicable. ^pa-vi-a-r3

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.VI.A.S1** Prepare and use a flight log. ^pa-vi-a-s1

- **PA.VI.A.S2** Navigate by pilotage. ^pa-vi-a-s2

- **PA.VI.A.S3** Navigate by means of pre-computed headings, groundspeeds, elapsed time, and reference to landmarks or checkpoints. ^pa-vi-a-s3

- **PA.VI.A.S4** Use the magnetic direction indicator in navigation, including turns to headings. ^pa-vi-a-s4

- **PA.VI.A.S5** Verify position within three nautical miles of the flight-planned route. ^pa-vi-a-s5

- **PA.VI.A.S6** Arrive at the en route checkpoints within five minutes of the initial or revised estimated time of arrival (ETA) and provide a destination estimate. ^pa-vi-a-s6

- **PA.VI.A.S7** Maintain the selected altitude, ±200 feet and heading, ±15°. ^pa-vi-a-s7
