# PA.XII.B — Seaplane Post-Landing Procedures (ASES, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=72)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.XII|Area of Operation XII — Postflight Procedures]] · **Applies to:** ASES, AMES

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with anchoring, docking, mooring, and ramping/beaching.

> [!note] Note
> The evaluator must select at least one after-landing procedure (anchoring, docking and mooring, or ramping/beaching).

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.XII.B.K1** Mooring. ^pa-xii-b-k1

- **PA.XII.B.K2** Docking. ^pa-xii-b-k2

- **PA.XII.B.K3** Anchoring. ^pa-xii-b-k3

- **PA.XII.B.K4** Beaching/ramping. ^pa-xii-b-k4

- **PA.XII.B.K5** Postflight inspection, recording of in-flight/postflight discrepancies. ^pa-xii-b-k5

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.XII.B.R1** Activities and distractions. ^pa-xii-b-r1

- **PA.XII.B.R2** \[Archived] ^pa-xii-b-r2

- **PA.XII.B.R3** Seaplane base specific security procedures, if applicable. ^pa-xii-b-r3

- **PA.XII.B.R4** Disembarking passengers safely on the ramp and monitoring passenger movement while on the ramp. ^pa-xii-b-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.XII.B.S1** If anchoring, select a suitable area considering seaplane movement, water depth, tide, wind, and weather changes. Use an adequate number of anchors and lines of sufficient strength and length to ensure the seaplane’s security. ^pa-xii-b-s1

- **PA.XII.B.S2** If not anchoring, approach the dock/mooring buoy or beach/ramp in the proper direction and at a safe speed, considering water depth, tide, current, and wind. ^pa-xii-b-s2

- **PA.XII.B.S3** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-xii-b-s3

- **PA.XII.B.S4** Conduct a postflight inspection and document discrepancies and servicing requirements, if any. ^pa-xii-b-s4

- **PA.XII.B.S5** Secure the seaplane considering the effect of wind, waves, and changes in water level, or comply with applicable after landing, parking, and securing procedures if operating an amphibious airplane on land. ^pa-xii-b-s5
