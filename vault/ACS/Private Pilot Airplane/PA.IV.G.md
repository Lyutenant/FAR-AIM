# PA.IV.G — Confined Area Takeoff and Maximum Performance Climb (ASES, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=34)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASES, AMES

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with confined area takeoff and maximum performance climb.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.G.K1** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on takeoff and climb performance. ^pa-iv-g-k1

- **PA.IV.G.K2** Best angle of climb speed (VX) and best rate of climb speed (VY). ^pa-iv-g-k2

- **PA.IV.G.K3** Appropriate airplane configuration. ^pa-iv-g-k3

- **PA.IV.G.K4** Effects of water surface. ^pa-iv-g-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.G.R1** Selection of takeoff path based on pilot capability, airplane performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-g-r1

- **PA.IV.G.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-g-r2

    - **PA.IV.G.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-g-r2a

    - **PA.IV.G.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-g-r2b

    - **PA.IV.G.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-g-r2c

    - **PA.IV.G.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-g-r2d

    - **PA.IV.G.R2e** e. Water surface/condition ^pa-iv-g-r2e

- **PA.IV.G.R3** Abnormal operations, including planning for: ^pa-iv-g-r3

    - **PA.IV.G.R3a** a. Rejected takeoff ^pa-iv-g-r3a

    - **PA.IV.G.R3b** b. Potential engine failure in takeoff/climb phase of flight ^pa-iv-g-r3b

- **PA.IV.G.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-g-r4

- **PA.IV.G.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-g-r5

- **PA.IV.G.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-g-r6

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.G.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-g-s1

- **PA.IV.G.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-g-s2

- **PA.IV.G.S3** Verify assigned/correct takeoff path. ^pa-iv-g-s3

- **PA.IV.G.S4** Determine wind direction with or without visible wind direction indicators. ^pa-iv-g-s4

- **PA.IV.G.S5** Position the flight controls for the existing wind, if applicable. ^pa-iv-g-s5

- **PA.IV.G.S6** Clear the area, taxi into takeoff position utilizing maximum available takeoff area, and align the airplane on the takeoff path. ^pa-iv-g-s6

    - **PA.IV.G.S6a** a. Retract the water rudders, as appropriate ^pa-iv-g-s6a

- **PA.IV.G.S7** Advance the throttle smoothly to takeoff power and confirm proper engine and flight instrument indications prior to rotation. ^pa-iv-g-s7

- **PA.IV.G.S8** Establish a pitch attitude that maintains the most efficient planing/lift-off attitude and correct for porpoising and skipping. ^pa-iv-g-s8

- **PA.IV.G.S9** Avoid excessive water spray on the propeller(s). ^pa-iv-g-s9

- **PA.IV.G.S10** Rotate and lift off at the recommended airspeed, and accelerate to the recommended obstacle clearance airspeed or VX. ^pa-iv-g-s10

- **PA.IV.G.S11** Establish a pitch attitude to maintain the recommended obstacle clearance airspeed or VX, +10/-5 knots until the obstacle is cleared or until the airplane is 50 feet above the surface. ^pa-iv-g-s11

- **PA.IV.G.S12** Establish a pitch attitude for VY and accelerate to VY +10/-5 knots after clearing the obstacle or at 50 feet AGL if simulating an obstacle. ^pa-iv-g-s12

- **PA.IV.G.S13** Retract flaps, if extended, after a positive rate of climb has been verified or in accordance with airplane manufacturer’s guidance. ^pa-iv-g-s13

- **PA.IV.G.S14** Maintain VY +10/-5 knots to a safe maneuvering altitude. ^pa-iv-g-s14

- **PA.IV.G.S15** Maintain directional control and proper wind-drift correction throughout takeoff and climb. ^pa-iv-g-s15

- **PA.IV.G.S16** Comply with noise abatement procedures, as applicable. ^pa-iv-g-s16
