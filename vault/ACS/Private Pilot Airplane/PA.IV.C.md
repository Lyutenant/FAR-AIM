# PA.IV.C — Soft-Field Takeoff and Climb (ASEL)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=28)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASEL

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with soft-field takeoff, climb operations, and rejected takeoff procedures.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.C.K1** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on takeoff and climb performance. ^pa-iv-c-k1

- **PA.IV.C.K2** Best angle of climb speed (VX) and best rate of climb speed (VY). ^pa-iv-c-k2

- **PA.IV.C.K3** Appropriate airplane configuration. ^pa-iv-c-k3

- **PA.IV.C.K4** Ground effect. ^pa-iv-c-k4

- **PA.IV.C.K5** Importance of weight transfer from wheels to wings. ^pa-iv-c-k5

- **PA.IV.C.K6** Left turning tendencies. ^pa-iv-c-k6

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.C.R1** Selection of runway based on pilot capability, airplane performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-c-r1

- **PA.IV.C.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-c-r2

    - **PA.IV.C.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-c-r2a

    - **PA.IV.C.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-c-r2b

    - **PA.IV.C.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-c-r2c

    - **PA.IV.C.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-c-r2d

    - **PA.IV.C.R2e** e. Takeoff surface/condition ^pa-iv-c-r2e

- **PA.IV.C.R3** Abnormal operations, including planning for: ^pa-iv-c-r3

    - **PA.IV.C.R3a** a. Rejected takeoff ^pa-iv-c-r3a

    - **PA.IV.C.R3b** b. Potential engine failure in takeoff/climb phase of flight ^pa-iv-c-r3b

- **PA.IV.C.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-c-r4

- **PA.IV.C.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-c-r5

- **PA.IV.C.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-c-r6

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.C.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-c-s1

- **PA.IV.C.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-c-s2

- **PA.IV.C.S3** Verify assigned/correct runway. ^pa-iv-c-s3

- **PA.IV.C.S4** Determine wind direction with or without visible wind direction indicators. ^pa-iv-c-s4

- **PA.IV.C.S5** Position the flight controls for the existing wind, if applicable. ^pa-iv-c-s5

- **PA.IV.C.S6** Clear the area, maintain necessary flight control inputs, taxi into takeoff position and align the airplane on the runway centerline without stopping, while advancing the throttle smoothly to takeoff power. ^pa-iv-c-s6

- **PA.IV.C.S7** Confirm takeoff power and proper engine and flight instrument indications. ^pa-iv-c-s7

- **PA.IV.C.S8** Establish and maintain a pitch attitude that transfers the weight of the airplane from the wheels to the wings as rapidly as possible. ^pa-iv-c-s8

- **PA.IV.C.S9** Lift off at the lowest possible airspeed and remain in ground effect while accelerating to VX or VY, as appropriate. ^pa-iv-c-s9

- **PA.IV.C.S10** Establish a pitch attitude for VX or VY, as appropriate, and maintain selected airspeed +10/-5 knots during the climb. ^pa-iv-c-s10

- **PA.IV.C.S11** Configure the airplane after a positive rate of climb has been verified or in accordance with airplane manufacturer’s instructions. ^pa-iv-c-s11

- **PA.IV.C.S12** Maintain VX or VY, as appropriate, +10/-5 knots to a safe maneuvering altitude. ^pa-iv-c-s12

- **PA.IV.C.S13** Maintain directional control and proper wind-drift correction throughout takeoff and climb. ^pa-iv-c-s13

- **PA.IV.C.S14** Comply with noise abatement procedures, as applicable. ^pa-iv-c-s14
