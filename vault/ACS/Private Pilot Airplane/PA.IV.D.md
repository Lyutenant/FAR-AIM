# PA.IV.D — Soft-Field Approach and Landing (ASEL)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=30)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASEL

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with soft-field approach and landing with emphasis on proper use and coordination of flight controls.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.D.K1** A stabilized approach, including energy management concepts. ^pa-iv-d-k1

- **PA.IV.D.K2** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on approach and landing performance. ^pa-iv-d-k2

- **PA.IV.D.K3** Wind correction techniques on approach and landing. ^pa-iv-d-k3

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.D.R1** Selection of runway based on pilot capability, airplane performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-d-r1

- **PA.IV.D.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-d-r2

    - **PA.IV.D.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-d-r2a

    - **PA.IV.D.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-d-r2b

    - **PA.IV.D.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-d-r2c

    - **PA.IV.D.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-d-r2d

    - **PA.IV.D.R2e** e. Landing surface/condition ^pa-iv-d-r2e

- **PA.IV.D.R3** Planning for: ^pa-iv-d-r3

    - **PA.IV.D.R3a** a. Rejected landing and go-around ^pa-iv-d-r3a

    - **PA.IV.D.R3b** b. Land and hold short operations (LAHSO) ^pa-iv-d-r3b

- **PA.IV.D.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-d-r4

- **PA.IV.D.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-d-r5

- **PA.IV.D.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-d-r6

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.D.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-d-s1

- **PA.IV.D.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-d-s2

- **PA.IV.D.S3** Ensure the airplane is aligned with the correct/assigned runway. ^pa-iv-d-s3

- **PA.IV.D.S4** Scan the landing runway and adjoining area for traffic and obstructions. ^pa-iv-d-s4

- **PA.IV.D.S5** Select and aim for a suitable touchdown point considering the wind conditions, landing surface, and obstructions. ^pa-iv-d-s5

- **PA.IV.D.S6** Establish the recommended approach and landing configuration, airspeed, and trim, and adjust pitch attitude and power as required to maintain a stabilized approach. ^pa-iv-d-s6

- **PA.IV.D.S7** Maintain manufacturer’s published approach airspeed or in its absence not more than 1.3 VSO, +10/-5 knots with gust factor applied. ^pa-iv-d-s7

- **PA.IV.D.S8** Maintain directional control and appropriate crosswind correction throughout the approach and landing. ^pa-iv-d-s8

- **PA.IV.D.S9** Make smooth, timely, and correct control inputs during the round out and touchdown, and, for tricycle gear airplanes, keep the nose wheel off the surface until loss of elevator effectiveness. ^pa-iv-d-s9

- **PA.IV.D.S10** Touch down at a proper pitch attitude with minimum sink rate, no side drift, and with the airplane’s longitudinal axis aligned with the center of the runway. ^pa-iv-d-s10

- **PA.IV.D.S11** Maintain elevator as recommended by manufacturer during rollout and exit the “soft” area at a speed that would preclude sinking into the surface. ^pa-iv-d-s11

- **PA.IV.D.S12** Execute a timely go-around if the approach cannot be made within the tolerances specified above or for any other condition that may result in an unsafe approach or landing. ^pa-iv-d-s12

- **PA.IV.D.S13** Maintain proper position of the flight controls and sufficient speed to taxi while on the soft surface. ^pa-iv-d-s13
