# PA.VII.D — Spin Awareness

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=53)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.VII|Area of Operation VII — Slow Flight and Stalls]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AC 61-67; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge of the causes and procedures for recovery from unintentional spins and understands the risk associated with unintentional spins.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.VII.D.K1** Aerodynamics associated with spins in various airplane configurations, including the relationship between angle of attack, airspeed, load factor, power setting, airplane weight and center of gravity, airplane attitude, and yaw effects. ^pa-vii-d-k1

- **PA.VII.D.K2** What causes a spin and how to identify the entry, incipient, and developed phases of a spin. ^pa-vii-d-k2

- **PA.VII.D.K3** Spin recovery procedure. ^pa-vii-d-k3

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.VII.D.R1** Factors and situations that could lead to inadvertent spin and loss of control. ^pa-vii-d-r1

- **PA.VII.D.R2** Range and limitations of stall warning indicators (e.g., aircraft buffet, stall horn, etc.). ^pa-vii-d-r2

- **PA.VII.D.R3** Spin recovery procedure. ^pa-vii-d-r3

- **PA.VII.D.R4** Effect of environmental elements on airplane performance related to spins (e.g., turbulence, microbursts, and high-density altitude). ^pa-vii-d-r4

- **PA.VII.D.R5** Collision hazards. ^pa-vii-d-r5

- **PA.VII.D.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-vii-d-r6
