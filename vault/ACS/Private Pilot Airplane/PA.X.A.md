# PA.X.A — Maneuvering with One Engine Inoperative (AMEL, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=66)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.X|Area of Operation X — Multiengine Operations]] · **Applies to:** AMEL, AMES

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; FAA-P-8740-66; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with maneuvering with one engine inoperative.

> [!note] Note
> See Appendix 2: Safety of Flight and Appendix 3: Aircraft, Equipment, and Operational Requirements & Limitations for information related to this Task.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.X.A.K1** Factors affecting minimum controllable speed (VMC). ^pa-x-a-k1

- **PA.X.A.K2** VMC (red line) and best single-engine rate of climb airspeed (VYSE) (blue line). ^pa-x-a-k2

- **PA.X.A.K3** How to identify, verify, feather, and secure an inoperative engine. ^pa-x-a-k3

- **PA.X.A.K4** Importance of drag reduction, including propeller feathering, gear and flap retraction, the manufacturer's recommended control input and its relation to zero sideslip. ^pa-x-a-k4

- **PA.X.A.K5** Feathering, securing, unfeathering, and restarting. ^pa-x-a-k5

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.X.A.R1** Potential engine failure during flight. ^pa-x-a-r1

- **PA.X.A.R2** Collision hazards. ^pa-x-a-r2

- **PA.X.A.R3** Configuring the airplane. ^pa-x-a-r3

- **PA.X.A.R4** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-x-a-r4

- **PA.X.A.R5** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-x-a-r5

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.X.A.S1** Recognize an engine failure, maintain control, use manufacturer’s memory item procedures, and use appropriate emergency procedures. ^pa-x-a-s1

- **PA.X.A.S2** Set the engine controls, identify and verify the inoperative engine, and feather the appropriate propeller. ^pa-x-a-s2

- **PA.X.A.S3** Use flight controls in the proper combination as recommended by the manufacturer, or as required to maintain best performance, and trim as required. ^pa-x-a-s3

- **PA.X.A.S4** Attempt to determine and resolve the reason for the engine failure. ^pa-x-a-s4

- **PA.X.A.S5** Secure the inoperative engine and monitor the operating engine and make necessary adjustments. ^pa-x-a-s5

- **PA.X.A.S6** Restart the inoperative engine using manufacturer’s restart procedures. ^pa-x-a-s6

- **PA.X.A.S7** Maintain altitude ±100 feet or minimum sink rate if applicable, airspeed ±10 knots, and selected headings ±10°. ^pa-x-a-s7

- **PA.X.A.S8** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-x-a-s8
