# PA.XI.A — Night Operations

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=71)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.XI|Area of Operation XI — Night Operations]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge and risk management associated with night operations.

> [!note] Note
> For applicants that reside in Alaska, refer to 14 CFR part 61, section 61.110.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.XI.A.K1** Physiological aspects of vision related to night flying. ^pa-xi-a-k1

- **PA.XI.A.K2** Lighting systems identifying airports, runways, taxiways and obstructions, as well as pilot controlled lighting. ^pa-xi-a-k2

- **PA.XI.A.K3** Airplane equipment and lighting requirements for night operations. ^pa-xi-a-k3

- **PA.XI.A.K4** Personal equipment essential for night flight. ^pa-xi-a-k4

- **PA.XI.A.K5** Night orientation, navigation, chart reading techniques and methods for maintaining night vision effectiveness. ^pa-xi-a-k5

- **PA.XI.A.K6** Night taxi operations. ^pa-xi-a-k6

- **PA.XI.A.K7** Interpretation of traffic position and direction based solely on position lights. ^pa-xi-a-k7

- **PA.XI.A.K8** Visual illusions at night. ^pa-xi-a-k8

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.XI.A.R1** Collision hazards. ^pa-xi-a-r1

- **PA.XI.A.R2** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-xi-a-r2

- **PA.XI.A.R3** Effect of visual illusions and night adaptation during all phases of night flying. ^pa-xi-a-r3

- **PA.XI.A.R4** Runway incursion. ^pa-xi-a-r4

- **PA.XI.A.R5** Night currency versus proficiency. ^pa-xi-a-r5

- **PA.XI.A.R6** Weather considerations specific to night operations. ^pa-xi-a-r6

- **PA.XI.A.R7** Inoperative equipment. ^pa-xi-a-r7
