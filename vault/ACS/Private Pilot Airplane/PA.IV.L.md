# PA.IV.L — Rough Water Approach and Landing (ASES, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=40)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASES, AMES

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with rough water approach and landing.

> [!note] Note
> If a rough water condition does not exist, the applicant must be evaluated by simulating the Task.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.L.K1** A stabilized approach, including energy management concepts. ^pa-iv-l-k1

- **PA.IV.L.K2** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on approach and landing performance. ^pa-iv-l-k2

- **PA.IV.L.K3** Wind correction techniques on approach and landing. ^pa-iv-l-k3

- **PA.IV.L.K4** When and why rough water techniques are used. ^pa-iv-l-k4

- **PA.IV.L.K5** How to perform a proper rough water approach and landing. ^pa-iv-l-k5

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.L.R1** Selection of approach path and touchdown area based on pilot capability, airplane performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-l-r1

- **PA.IV.L.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-l-r2

    - **PA.IV.L.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-l-r2a

    - **PA.IV.L.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-l-r2b

    - **PA.IV.L.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-l-r2c

    - **PA.IV.L.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-l-r2d

    - **PA.IV.L.R2e** e. Water surface/condition ^pa-iv-l-r2e

- **PA.IV.L.R3** Planning for a go-around and rejected landing. ^pa-iv-l-r3

- **PA.IV.L.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-l-r4

- **PA.IV.L.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-l-r5

- **PA.IV.L.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-l-r6

- **PA.IV.L.R7** Gear position in an amphibious airplane. ^pa-iv-l-r7

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.L.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-l-s1

- **PA.IV.L.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-l-s2

- **PA.IV.L.S3** Ensure the airplane is aligned with the correct/assigned waterway. ^pa-iv-l-s3

- **PA.IV.L.S4** Scan the landing area for traffic and obstructions. ^pa-iv-l-s4

- **PA.IV.L.S5** Select and aim for a suitable touchdown point considering the wind conditions, landing surface, and obstructions. ^pa-iv-l-s5

- **PA.IV.L.S6** Establish the recommended approach and landing configuration, airspeed, and trim, and adjust pitch attitude and power as required to maintain a stabilized approach. ^pa-iv-l-s6

- **PA.IV.L.S7** Maintain manufacturer’s published approach airspeed or in its absence not more than 1.3 VSO, +10/-5 knots with gust factor applied. ^pa-iv-l-s7

- **PA.IV.L.S8** Maintain directional control and appropriate crosswind correction throughout the approach and landing. ^pa-iv-l-s8

- **PA.IV.L.S9** Make smooth, timely, and correct power and control adjustments to maintain proper pitch attitude and rate of descent to touchdown. ^pa-iv-l-s9

- **PA.IV.L.S10** Contact the water in a proper pitch attitude, considering the type of rough water. ^pa-iv-l-s10
