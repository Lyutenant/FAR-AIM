# PA.I.I — Water and Seaplane Characteristics, Seaplane Bases, Maritime Rules, and Aids to Marine Navigation (ASES, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=17)

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with water and seaplane characteristics, seaplane bases, maritime rules, and aids to marine navigation.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.I.I.K1** The characteristics of a water surface as affected by features, such as: ^pa-i-i-k1

    - **PA.I.I.K1a** a. Size and location ^pa-i-i-k1a

    - **PA.I.I.K1b** b. Protected and unprotected areas ^pa-i-i-k1b

    - **PA.I.I.K1c** c. Surface wind ^pa-i-i-k1c

    - **PA.I.I.K1d** d. Direction and strength of water current ^pa-i-i-k1d

    - **PA.I.I.K1e** e. Floating and partially submerged debris ^pa-i-i-k1e

    - **PA.I.I.K1f** f. Sandbars, islands, and shoals ^pa-i-i-k1f

    - **PA.I.I.K1g** g. Vessel traffic and wakes ^pa-i-i-k1g

    - **PA.I.I.K1h** h. Other characteristics specific to the area ^pa-i-i-k1h

    - **PA.I.I.K1i** i. Direction and height of waves ^pa-i-i-k1i

- **PA.I.I.K2** Float and hull construction, and its effect on seaplane performance. ^pa-i-i-k2

- **PA.I.I.K3** Causes of porpoising and skipping, and the pilot action needed to prevent or correct these occurrences. ^pa-i-i-k3

- **PA.I.I.K4** How to locate and identify seaplane bases on charts or in directories. ^pa-i-i-k4

- **PA.I.I.K5** Operating restrictions at various bases. ^pa-i-i-k5

- **PA.I.I.K6** Right-of-way, steering, and sailing rules pertinent to seaplane operation. ^pa-i-i-k6

- **PA.I.I.K7** Marine navigation aids, such as buoys, beacons, lights, sound signals, and range markers. ^pa-i-i-k7

- **PA.I.I.K8** Naval vessel protection zones. ^pa-i-i-k8

- **PA.I.I.K9** No wake zones. ^pa-i-i-k9

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.I.I.R1** Local conditions. ^pa-i-i-r1

- **PA.I.I.R2** Impact of marine traffic. ^pa-i-i-r2

- **PA.I.I.R3** Right-of-way and sailing rules pertinent to seaplane operations. ^pa-i-i-r3

- **PA.I.I.R4** Limited services and assistance available at seaplane bases. ^pa-i-i-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.I.I.S1** Assess the water surface characteristics for the proposed flight. ^pa-i-i-s1

- **PA.I.I.S2** Identify restrictions at local seaplane bases. ^pa-i-i-s2

- **PA.I.I.S3** Identify marine navigation aids. ^pa-i-i-s3

- **PA.I.I.S4** Describe correct right-of-way, steering, and sailing operations. ^pa-i-i-s4

- **PA.I.I.S5** Explain how float and hull construction can affect seaplane performance. ^pa-i-i-s5

- **PA.I.I.S6** Describe how to correct for porpoising and skipping. ^pa-i-i-s6
