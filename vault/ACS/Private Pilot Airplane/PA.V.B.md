# PA.V.B — Ground Reference Maneuvers

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=44)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.V|Area of Operation V — Performance Maneuvers and Ground Reference Maneuvers]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

14 CFR part 61; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25

- [[Part 61]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with ground reference maneuvering which may include a rectangular course, S-turns, and turns around a point.

> [!note] Note
> The evaluator selects at least one ground reference maneuver for the applicant to demonstrate.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.V.B.K1** Purpose of ground reference maneuvers. ^pa-v-b-k1

- **PA.V.B.K2** Effects of wind on ground track and relation to a ground reference. ^pa-v-b-k2

- **PA.V.B.K3** Effects of bank angle and groundspeed on rate and radius of turn. ^pa-v-b-k3

- **PA.V.B.K4** Relationship of rectangular course to airport traffic pattern. ^pa-v-b-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.V.B.R1** Division of attention between aircraft control and orientation. ^pa-v-b-r1

- **PA.V.B.R2** Collision hazards. ^pa-v-b-r2

- **PA.V.B.R3** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-v-b-r3

- **PA.V.B.R4** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-v-b-r4

- **PA.V.B.R5** Uncoordinated flight. ^pa-v-b-r5

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.V.B.S1** Clear the area. ^pa-v-b-s1

- **PA.V.B.S2** Select a suitable ground reference area, line, or point as appropriate. ^pa-v-b-s2

- **PA.V.B.S3** Plan the maneuver: ^pa-v-b-s3

    - **PA.V.B.S3a** a. Rectangular course: enter a left or right pattern, 600 to 1,000 feet above ground level (AGL) at an appropriate distance from the selected reference area, 45° to the downwind leg ^pa-v-b-s3a

    - **PA.V.B.S3b** b. S-turns: enter perpendicular to the selected reference line, 600 to 1,000 feet AGL at an appropriate distance from the selected reference area ^pa-v-b-s3b

    - **PA.V.B.S3c** c. Turns around a point: enter at an appropriate distance from the reference point, 600 to 1,000 feet AGL at an appropriate distance from the selected reference area ^pa-v-b-s3c

- **PA.V.B.S4** Apply adequate wind-drift correction during straight and turning flight to maintain a constant ground track around a rectangular reference area, or to maintain a constant radius turn on each side of a selected reference line or point. ^pa-v-b-s4

- **PA.V.B.S5** If performing S-Turns, reverse the turn directly over the selected reference line; if performing turns around a point, complete turns in either direction, as specified by the evaluator. ^pa-v-b-s5

- **PA.V.B.S6** Divide attention between airplane control, traffic avoidance and the ground track while maintaining coordinated flight. ^pa-v-b-s6

- **PA.V.B.S7** Maintain altitude ±100 feet; maintain airspeed ±10 knots. ^pa-v-b-s7
