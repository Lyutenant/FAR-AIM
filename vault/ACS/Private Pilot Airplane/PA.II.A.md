# PA.II.A — Preflight Assessment

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=19)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.II|Area of Operation II — Preflight Procedures]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25, FAA-H-8083-28; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with preparation for safe flight.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.II.A.K1** Pilot self-assessment. ^pa-ii-a-k1

- **PA.II.A.K2** Determining that the airplane to be used is appropriate and airworthy. ^pa-ii-a-k2

- **PA.II.A.K3** Airplane preflight inspection, including: ^pa-ii-a-k3

    - **PA.II.A.K3a** a. Which items should be inspected ^pa-ii-a-k3a

    - **PA.II.A.K3b** b. The reasons for checking each item ^pa-ii-a-k3b

    - **PA.II.A.K3c** c. How to detect possible defects ^pa-ii-a-k3c

    - **PA.II.A.K3d** d. The associated regulations ^pa-ii-a-k3d

- **PA.II.A.K4** Environmental factors, including weather, terrain, route selection, and obstructions. ^pa-ii-a-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.II.A.R1** Pilot. ^pa-ii-a-r1

- **PA.II.A.R2** Aircraft. ^pa-ii-a-r2

- **PA.II.A.R3** Environment (e.g., weather, airports, airspace, terrain, obstacles). ^pa-ii-a-r3

- **PA.II.A.R4** External pressures. ^pa-ii-a-r4

- **PA.II.A.R5** Aviation security concerns. ^pa-ii-a-r5

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.II.A.S1** Inspect the airplane with reference to an appropriate checklist. ^pa-ii-a-s1

- **PA.II.A.S2** Verify the airplane is in condition for safe flight and conforms to its type design. ^pa-ii-a-s2

- **PA.II.A.S3** Perform self-assessment. ^pa-ii-a-s3

- **PA.II.A.S4** Continue to assess the environment for safe flight. ^pa-ii-a-s4
