# PA.IV.N — Go-Around/Rejected Landing

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=42)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with go-around/rejected landing with emphasis on factors that contribute to landing conditions that may require a go-around.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.N.K1** A stabilized approach, including energy management concepts. ^pa-iv-n-k1

- **PA.IV.N.K2** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind and density altitude, on a go-around or rejected landing. ^pa-iv-n-k2

- **PA.IV.N.K3** Wind correction techniques on takeoff/departure and approach/landing. ^pa-iv-n-k3

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.N.R1** Delayed recognition of the need for a go-around/rejected landing. ^pa-iv-n-r1

- **PA.IV.N.R2** Delayed performance of a go-around at low altitude. ^pa-iv-n-r2

- **PA.IV.N.R3** Power application. ^pa-iv-n-r3

- **PA.IV.N.R4** Configuring the airplane. ^pa-iv-n-r4

- **PA.IV.N.R5** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-n-r5

- **PA.IV.N.R6** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-n-r6

- **PA.IV.N.R7** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-n-r7

- **PA.IV.N.R8** Runway incursion. ^pa-iv-n-r8

- **PA.IV.N.R9** Managing a go-around/rejected landing after accepting a LAHSO clearance. ^pa-iv-n-r9

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.N.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-n-s1

- **PA.IV.N.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-n-s2

- **PA.IV.N.S3** Make a timely decision to discontinue the approach to landing. ^pa-iv-n-s3

- **PA.IV.N.S4** Apply takeoff power immediately and transition to climb pitch attitude for VX or VY as appropriate +10/-5 knots. ^pa-iv-n-s4

- **PA.IV.N.S5** Configure the airplane after a positive rate of climb has been verified or in accordance with airplane manufacturer’s instructions. ^pa-iv-n-s5

- **PA.IV.N.S6** Maneuver to the side of the runway/landing area when necessary to clear and avoid conflicting traffic. ^pa-iv-n-s6

- **PA.IV.N.S7** Maintain VY +10/-5 knots to a safe maneuvering altitude. ^pa-iv-n-s7

- **PA.IV.N.S8** Maintain directional control and proper wind-drift correction throughout the climb. ^pa-iv-n-s8

- **PA.IV.N.S9** Use runway incursion avoidance procedures, if applicable. ^pa-iv-n-s9
