# PA.I.B — Airworthiness Requirements

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=10)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.I|Area of Operation I — Preflight Preparation]] · **Applies to:** all classes

14 CFR parts 39, 43, 91; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25

- [[Part 43]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with airworthiness requirements, including airplane certificates.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.I.B.K1** General airworthiness requirements and compliance for airplanes, including: ^pa-i-b-k1

    - **PA.I.B.K1a** a. Location and expiration dates of required aircraft certificates ^pa-i-b-k1a

    - **PA.I.B.K1b** b. Required inspections and airplane logbook documentation ^pa-i-b-k1b

    - **PA.I.B.K1c** c. Airworthiness Directives and Special Airworthiness Information Bulletins ^pa-i-b-k1c

    - **PA.I.B.K1d** d. Purpose and procedure for obtaining a special flight permit ^pa-i-b-k1d

    - **PA.I.B.K1e** e. Owner/Operator and pilot-in-command responsibilities ^pa-i-b-k1e

- **PA.I.B.K2** Pilot-performed preventive maintenance. ^pa-i-b-k2

- **PA.I.B.K3** Equipment requirements for day and night VFR flight, including: ^pa-i-b-k3

    - **PA.I.B.K3a** a. Flying with inoperative equipment ^pa-i-b-k3a

    - **PA.I.B.K3b** b. Using an approved Minimum Equipment List (MEL) ^pa-i-b-k3b

    - **PA.I.B.K3c** c. Kinds of Operation Equipment List (KOEL) ^pa-i-b-k3c

    - **PA.I.B.K3d** d. Required discrepancy records or placards ^pa-i-b-k3d

- **PA.I.B.K4** Standard and special airworthiness certificates and their associated operational limitations. ^pa-i-b-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.I.B.R1** Inoperative equipment discovered prior to flight. ^pa-i-b-r1

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.I.B.S1** Locate and describe airplane airworthiness and registration information. ^pa-i-b-s1

- **PA.I.B.S2** Determine the airplane is airworthy in the scenario given by the evaluator. ^pa-i-b-s2

- **PA.I.B.S3** Apply appropriate procedures for operating with inoperative equipment in the scenario given by the evaluator. ^pa-i-b-s3
