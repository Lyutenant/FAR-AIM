# PA.I.G — Operation of Systems

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=15)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.I|Area of Operation I — Preflight Preparation]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with safe operation of systems on the airplane provided for the flight test.

> [!note] Note
> If K1 is selected, the evaluator must assess the applicant's knowledge of at least three sub-elements.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.I.G.K1** Airplane systems, including: ^pa-i-g-k1

    - **PA.I.G.K1a** a. Primary flight controls ^pa-i-g-k1a

    - **PA.I.G.K1b** b. Secondary flight controls ^pa-i-g-k1b

    - **PA.I.G.K1c** c. Powerplant and propeller ^pa-i-g-k1c

    - **PA.I.G.K1d** d. Landing gear ^pa-i-g-k1d

    - **PA.I.G.K1e** e. Fuel, oil, and hydraulic ^pa-i-g-k1e

    - **PA.I.G.K1f** f. Electrical ^pa-i-g-k1f

    - **PA.I.G.K1g** g. Avionics ^pa-i-g-k1g

    - **PA.I.G.K1h** h. Pitot-static, vacuum/pressure, and associated flight instruments ^pa-i-g-k1h

    - **PA.I.G.K1i** i. Environmental ^pa-i-g-k1i

    - **PA.I.G.K1j** j. Deicing and anti-icing ^pa-i-g-k1j

    - **PA.I.G.K1k** k. Water rudders (ASES, AMES) ^pa-i-g-k1k

    - **PA.I.G.K1l** l. Oxygen system ^pa-i-g-k1l

- **PA.I.G.K2** Indications of and procedures for managing system abnormalities or failures. ^pa-i-g-k2

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.I.G.R1** Detection of system malfunctions or failures. ^pa-i-g-r1

- **PA.I.G.R2** Management of a system failure. ^pa-i-g-r2

- **PA.I.G.R3** Monitoring and management of automated systems. ^pa-i-g-r3

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.I.G.S1** Operate at least three of the systems listed in K1a through K1l appropriately. ^pa-i-g-s1

- **PA.I.G.S2** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-i-g-s2
