# PA.I.D — Cross-Country Flight Planning

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=12)

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with cross-country flights and VFR flight planning.

> [!note] Note
> Preparation, presentation, and explanation of a computer-generated flight plan is an acceptable option.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.I.D.K1** Route planning, including consideration of different classes and special use airspace (SUA) and selection of appropriate and available navigation/communication systems and facilities. ^pa-i-d-k1

    - **PA.I.D.K1a** a. Use of an electronic flight bag (EFB), if used ^pa-i-d-k1a

- **PA.I.D.K2** Altitude selection accounting for terrain and obstacles, glide distance of airplane, VFR cruising altitudes, and effect of wind. ^pa-i-d-k2

- **PA.I.D.K3** Calculating: ^pa-i-d-k3

    - **PA.I.D.K3a** a. Time, climb and descent rates, course, distance, heading, true airspeed, and groundspeed ^pa-i-d-k3a

    - **PA.I.D.K3b** b. Estimated time of arrival, including conversion to universal coordinated time (UTC) ^pa-i-d-k3b

    - **PA.I.D.K3c** c. Fuel requirements, including reserve ^pa-i-d-k3c

- **PA.I.D.K4** Elements of a VFR flight plan. ^pa-i-d-k4

- **PA.I.D.K5** Procedures for filing, activating, and closing a VFR flight plan. ^pa-i-d-k5

- **PA.I.D.K6** Inflight intercept procedures. ^pa-i-d-k6

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.I.D.R1** Pilot. ^pa-i-d-r1

- **PA.I.D.R2** Aircraft. ^pa-i-d-r2

- **PA.I.D.R3** Environment (e.g., weather, airports, airspace, terrain, obstacles). ^pa-i-d-r3

- **PA.I.D.R4** External pressures. ^pa-i-d-r4

- **PA.I.D.R5** Limitations of air traffic control (ATC) services. ^pa-i-d-r5

- **PA.I.D.R6** Fuel planning. ^pa-i-d-r6

- **PA.I.D.R7** Use of an electronic flight bag (EFB), if used. ^pa-i-d-r7

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.I.D.S1** Prepare, present, and explain a cross-country flight plan assigned by the evaluator, including a risk analysis based on real-time weather, to the first fuel stop. ^pa-i-d-s1

- **PA.I.D.S2** Apply pertinent information from appropriate and current aeronautical charts, Chart Supplements; Notices to Air Missions (NOTAMs) relative to airport, runway and taxiway closures; and other flight publications. ^pa-i-d-s2

- **PA.I.D.S3** Create a navigation plan and simulate filing a VFR flight plan. ^pa-i-d-s3

- **PA.I.D.S4** Recalculate fuel reserves based on a scenario provided by the evaluator. ^pa-i-d-s4

- **PA.I.D.S5** Use an electronic flight bag (EFB), if applicable. ^pa-i-d-s5
