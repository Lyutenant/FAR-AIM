# PA.IV.I — Glassy Water Takeoff and Climb (ASES, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=36)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASES, AMES

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with glassy water takeoff and climb.

> [!note] Note
> If a glassy water condition does not exist, the applicant must be evaluated by simulating the Task.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.I.K1** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on takeoff and climb performance. ^pa-iv-i-k1

- **PA.IV.I.K2** Best angle of climb speed (VX) and best rate of climb speed (VY). ^pa-iv-i-k2

- **PA.IV.I.K3** Appropriate airplane configuration. ^pa-iv-i-k3

- **PA.IV.I.K4** Appropriate use of glassy water takeoff and climb technique. ^pa-iv-i-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.I.R1** Selection of takeoff path based on pilot capability, airplane performance and limitations, and available distance. ^pa-iv-i-r1

- **PA.IV.I.R2** Water surface/condition. ^pa-iv-i-r2

- **PA.IV.I.R3** Abnormal operations, including planning for: ^pa-iv-i-r3

    - **PA.IV.I.R3a** a. Rejected takeoff ^pa-iv-i-r3a

    - **PA.IV.I.R3b** b. Potential engine failure in takeoff/climb phase of flight ^pa-iv-i-r3b

- **PA.IV.I.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-i-r4

- **PA.IV.I.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-i-r5

- **PA.IV.I.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-i-r6

- **PA.IV.I.R7** Gear position in an amphibious airplane. ^pa-iv-i-r7

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.I.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-i-s1

- **PA.IV.I.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-i-s2

- **PA.IV.I.S3** Position flight controls and configure the aircraft for the existing conditions. ^pa-iv-i-s3

- **PA.IV.I.S4** Clear the area, select appropriate takeoff path considering surface hazards or vessels and surface conditions: ^pa-iv-i-s4

    - **PA.IV.I.S4a** a. Retract the water rudders, as appropriate ^pa-iv-i-s4a

    - **PA.IV.I.S4b** b. Advance the throttle smoothly to takeoff power and confirm proper engine and flight instrument indications prior to rotation ^pa-iv-i-s4b

- **PA.IV.I.S5** \[Archived] ^pa-iv-i-s5

- **PA.IV.I.S6** Establish and maintain an appropriate planing attitude, directional control, and correct for porpoising, skipping, and increase in water drag. ^pa-iv-i-s6

- **PA.IV.I.S7** Avoid excessive water spray on the propeller(s). ^pa-iv-i-s7

- **PA.IV.I.S8** Use appropriate techniques to lift seaplane from the water considering surface conditions. ^pa-iv-i-s8

- **PA.IV.I.S9** Establish proper attitude/airspeed and accelerate to VY +10/-5 knots during the climb. ^pa-iv-i-s9

- **PA.IV.I.S10** Configure the airplane after a positive rate of climb has been verified or in accordance with airplane manufacturer’s instructions. ^pa-iv-i-s10

- **PA.IV.I.S11** Maintain VY +10/-5 knots to a safe maneuvering altitude. ^pa-iv-i-s11

- **PA.IV.I.S12** Maintain directional control throughout takeoff and climb. ^pa-iv-i-s12
