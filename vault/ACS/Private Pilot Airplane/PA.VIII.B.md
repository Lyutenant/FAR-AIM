# PA.VIII.B — Constant Airspeed Climbs

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=54)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.VIII|Area of Operation VIII — Basic Instrument Maneuvers]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-15, FAA-H-8083-25

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with attitude instrument flying during constant airspeed climbs solely by reference to instruments.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.VIII.B.K1** Flight instruments as they relate to: ^pa-viii-b-k1

    - **PA.VIII.B.K1a** a. Instrument limitations and potential errors ^pa-viii-b-k1a

    - **PA.VIII.B.K1b** b. Indication of the aircraft attitude ^pa-viii-b-k1b

    - **PA.VIII.B.K1c** c. Function and operation ^pa-viii-b-k1c

    - **PA.VIII.B.K1d** d. Proper instrument cross-check techniques ^pa-viii-b-k1d

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.VIII.B.R1** Instrument flying hazards, including failure to maintain visual flight rules (VFR), spatial disorientation, loss of control, fatigue, stress, and emergency off airport landings. ^pa-viii-b-r1

- **PA.VIII.B.R2** When to seek assistance or declare an emergency in a deteriorating situation. ^pa-viii-b-r2

- **PA.VIII.B.R3** Collision hazards. ^pa-viii-b-r3

- **PA.VIII.B.R4** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-viii-b-r4

- **PA.VIII.B.R5** Fixation and omission. ^pa-viii-b-r5

- **PA.VIII.B.R6** Instrument Interpretation. ^pa-viii-b-r6

- **PA.VIII.B.R7** Control application solely by reference to instruments. ^pa-viii-b-r7

- **PA.VIII.B.R8** Trimming the aircraft. ^pa-viii-b-r8

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.VIII.B.S1** Transition to the climb pitch attitude and power setting on an assigned heading using proper instrument cross-check and interpretation, and coordinated flight control application. ^pa-viii-b-s1

- **PA.VIII.B.S2** Climb at a constant airspeed to specific altitudes in straight flight and turns. ^pa-viii-b-s2

- **PA.VIII.B.S3** Level off at the assigned altitude and maintain altitude ±200 feet, heading ±20°, and airspeed ±10 knots. ^pa-viii-b-s3
