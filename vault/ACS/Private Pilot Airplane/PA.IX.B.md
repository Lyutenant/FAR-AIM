# PA.IX.B — Emergency Approach and Landing (Simulated) (ASEL, ASES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=59)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IX|Area of Operation IX — Emergency Operations]] · **Applies to:** ASEL, ASES

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with emergency approach and landing procedures.

> [!note] Note
> See Appendix 2: Safety of Flight.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IX.B.K1** Immediate action items and emergency procedures. ^pa-ix-b-k1

- **PA.IX.B.K2** Airspeed, including: ^pa-ix-b-k2

    - **PA.IX.B.K2a** a. Importance of best glide speed and its relationship to distance ^pa-ix-b-k2a

    - **PA.IX.B.K2b** b. Difference between best glide speed and minimum sink speed ^pa-ix-b-k2b

    - **PA.IX.B.K2c** c. Effects of wind on glide distance ^pa-ix-b-k2c

- **PA.IX.B.K3** Effects of atmospheric conditions on emergency approach and landing. ^pa-ix-b-k3

- **PA.IX.B.K4** A stabilized approach, including energy management concepts. ^pa-ix-b-k4

- **PA.IX.B.K5** Emergency Locator Transmitters (ELTs) and other emergency locating devices. ^pa-ix-b-k5

- **PA.IX.B.K6** Air traffic control (ATC) services to aircraft in distress. ^pa-ix-b-k6

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IX.B.R1** Altitude, wind, terrain, obstructions, gliding distance, and available landing distance considerations. ^pa-ix-b-r1

- **PA.IX.B.R2** Following or changing the planned flightpath to the selected landing area. ^pa-ix-b-r2

- **PA.IX.B.R3** Collision hazards. ^pa-ix-b-r3

- **PA.IX.B.R4** Configuring the airplane. ^pa-ix-b-r4

- **PA.IX.B.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-ix-b-r5

- **PA.IX.B.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-ix-b-r6

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IX.B.S1** Establish and maintain the recommended best glide airspeed, ±10 knots. ^pa-ix-b-s1

- **PA.IX.B.S2** Configure the airplane in accordance with the Pilot's Operating Handbook (POH)\\Airplane Flight Manual (AFM) and existing conditions. ^pa-ix-b-s2

- **PA.IX.B.S3** Select a suitable landing area considering altitude, wind, terrain, obstructions, and available glide distance. ^pa-ix-b-s3

- **PA.IX.B.S4** Plan and follow a flightpath to the selected landing area considering altitude, wind, terrain, and obstructions. ^pa-ix-b-s4

- **PA.IX.B.S5** Prepare for landing as specified by the evaluator. ^pa-ix-b-s5

- **PA.IX.B.S6** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-ix-b-s6
