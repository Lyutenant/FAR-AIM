# PA.IV.A — Normal Takeoff and Climb

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=26)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with normal takeoff, climb operations, and rejected takeoff procedures.

> [!note] Note
> If a crosswind condition does not exist, the applicant’s knowledge of crosswind elements must be evaluated through oral testing.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.A.K1** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on takeoff and climb performance. ^pa-iv-a-k1

- **PA.IV.A.K2** Best angle of climb speed (VX) and best rate of climb speed (VY). ^pa-iv-a-k2

- **PA.IV.A.K3** Appropriate airplane configuration. ^pa-iv-a-k3

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.A.R1** Selection of runway or takeoff path based on aircraft performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-a-r1

- **PA.IV.A.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-a-r2

    - **PA.IV.A.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-a-r2a

    - **PA.IV.A.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-a-r2b

    - **PA.IV.A.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-a-r2c

    - **PA.IV.A.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-a-r2d

    - **PA.IV.A.R2e** e. Takeoff surface/condition ^pa-iv-a-r2e

- **PA.IV.A.R3** Abnormal operations, including planning for: ^pa-iv-a-r3

    - **PA.IV.A.R3a** a. Rejected takeoff ^pa-iv-a-r3a

    - **PA.IV.A.R3b** b. Potential engine failure in takeoff/climb phase of flight ^pa-iv-a-r3b

- **PA.IV.A.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-a-r4

- **PA.IV.A.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-a-r5

- **PA.IV.A.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-a-r6

- **PA.IV.A.R7** Runway incursion. ^pa-iv-a-r7

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.A.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-a-s1

- **PA.IV.A.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-a-s2

- **PA.IV.A.S3** Verify assigned/correct runway or takeoff path. ^pa-iv-a-s3

- **PA.IV.A.S4** Determine wind direction with or without visible wind direction indicators. ^pa-iv-a-s4

- **PA.IV.A.S5** Position the flight controls for the existing wind, if applicable. ^pa-iv-a-s5

- **PA.IV.A.S6** Clear the area, taxi into takeoff position, and align the airplane on the runway centerline (ASEL, AMEL) or takeoff path (ASES, AMES). ^pa-iv-a-s6

    - **PA.IV.A.S6a** a. Retract the water rudders, as appropriate (ASES, AMES) ^pa-iv-a-s6a

- **PA.IV.A.S7** Advance the throttle smoothly to takeoff power and confirm proper engine and flight instrument indications prior to rotation. ^pa-iv-a-s7

    - **PA.IV.A.S7a** a. Establish and maintain the most efficient planing/lift-off attitude, and correct for porpoising or skipping (ASES, AMES) ^pa-iv-a-s7a

- **PA.IV.A.S8** Avoid excessive water spray on the propeller(s) (ASES, AMES). ^pa-iv-a-s8

- **PA.IV.A.S9** Rotate and lift off at the recommended airspeed and accelerate to VY. ^pa-iv-a-s9

- **PA.IV.A.S10** \[Archived] ^pa-iv-a-s10

- **PA.IV.A.S11** Establish a pitch attitude to maintain the manufacturer’s recommended speed or VY, +10/-5 knots. ^pa-iv-a-s11

- **PA.IV.A.S12** Configure the airplane in accordance with manufacturer’s guidance. ^pa-iv-a-s12

- **PA.IV.A.S13** Maintain VY +10/-5 knots to a safe maneuvering altitude. ^pa-iv-a-s13

- **PA.IV.A.S14** Maintain directional control and proper wind-drift correction throughout takeoff and climb. ^pa-iv-a-s14

- **PA.IV.A.S15** Comply with noise abatement procedures, as applicable. ^pa-iv-a-s15
