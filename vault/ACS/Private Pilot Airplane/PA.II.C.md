# PA.II.C — Engine Starting

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=20)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.II|Area of Operation II — Preflight Procedures]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with recommended engine starting procedures.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.II.C.K1** Starting under various conditions. ^pa-ii-c-k1

- **PA.II.C.K2** Starting the engine(s) by use of external power. ^pa-ii-c-k2

- **PA.II.C.K3** Limitations associated with starting. ^pa-ii-c-k3

- **PA.II.C.K4** Conditions leading to and procedures for an aborted start. ^pa-ii-c-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.II.C.R1** Propeller safety. ^pa-ii-c-r1

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.II.C.S1** Position the airplane properly considering structures, other aircraft, wind, and the safety of nearby persons and property. ^pa-ii-c-s1

- **PA.II.C.S2** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-ii-c-s2
