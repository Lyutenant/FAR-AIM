# PA.IX.A — Emergency Descent

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=59)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IX|Area of Operation IX — Emergency Operations]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with emergency descent.

> [!note] Note
> See Appendix 2: Safety of Flight.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IX.A.K1** Situations that would require an emergency descent (e.g., depressurization, smoke, or engine fire). ^pa-ix-a-k1

- **PA.IX.A.K2** Immediate action items and emergency procedures. ^pa-ix-a-k2

- **PA.IX.A.K3** Airspeed, including airspeed limitations. ^pa-ix-a-k3

- **PA.IX.A.K4** Aircraft performance and limitations. ^pa-ix-a-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IX.A.R1** Altitude, wind, terrain, obstructions, gliding distance, and available landing distance considerations. ^pa-ix-a-r1

- **PA.IX.A.R2** Collision hazards. ^pa-ix-a-r2

- **PA.IX.A.R3** Configuring the airplane. ^pa-ix-a-r3

- **PA.IX.A.R4** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-ix-a-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IX.A.S1** Clear the area. ^pa-ix-a-s1

- **PA.IX.A.S2** Establish and maintain the appropriate airspeed and configuration appropriate to the scenario specified by the evaluator and as covered in Pilot's Operating Handbook (POH)/Airplane Flight Manual (AFM) for the emergency descent. ^pa-ix-a-s2

- **PA.IX.A.S3** Maintain orientation, divide attention appropriately, and plan and execute a smooth recovery. ^pa-ix-a-s3

- **PA.IX.A.S4** Use bank angle between 30° and 45° to maintain positive load factors during the descent. ^pa-ix-a-s4

- **PA.IX.A.S5** Maintain appropriate airspeed +0/-10 knots, and level off at a specified altitude ±100 feet. ^pa-ix-a-s5

- **PA.IX.A.S6** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-ix-a-s6

- **PA.IX.A.S7** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-ix-a-s7

- **PA.IX.A.S8** Use single-pilot resource management (SRM) or crew resource management (CRM), as appropriate. ^pa-ix-a-s8
