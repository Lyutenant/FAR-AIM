# PA.II.D — Taxiing (ASEL, AMEL)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=21)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.II|Area of Operation II — Preflight Procedures]] · **Applies to:** ASEL, AMEL

## References

AC 91-73; AIM; Chart Supplements; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with taxi operations, including runway incursion avoidance.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.II.D.K1** Current airport aeronautical references and information resources such as the Chart Supplement, airport diagram, and Notices to Air Missions (NOTAMs). ^pa-ii-d-k1

- **PA.II.D.K2** Taxi instructions/clearances. ^pa-ii-d-k2

- **PA.II.D.K3** Airport markings, signs, and lights. ^pa-ii-d-k3

- **PA.II.D.K4** Visual indicators for wind. ^pa-ii-d-k4

- **PA.II.D.K5** Aircraft lighting, as appropriate. ^pa-ii-d-k5

- **PA.II.D.K6** Procedures for: ^pa-ii-d-k6

    - **PA.II.D.K6a** a. Appropriate flight deck activities prior to taxi, including route planning and identifying the location of Hot Spots ^pa-ii-d-k6a

    - **PA.II.D.K6b** b. Radio communications at towered and nontowered airports ^pa-ii-d-k6b

    - **PA.II.D.K6c** c. Entering or crossing runways ^pa-ii-d-k6c

    - **PA.II.D.K6d** d. Night taxi operations ^pa-ii-d-k6d

    - **PA.II.D.K6e** e. Low visibility taxi operations ^pa-ii-d-k6e

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.II.D.R1** Activities and distractions. ^pa-ii-d-r1

- **PA.II.D.R2** Confirmation or expectation bias as related to taxi instructions. ^pa-ii-d-r2

- **PA.II.D.R3** A taxi route or departure runway change. ^pa-ii-d-r3

- **PA.II.D.R4** Runway incursion. ^pa-ii-d-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.II.D.S1** Receive and correctly read back clearances/instructions, if applicable. ^pa-ii-d-s1

- **PA.II.D.S2** Use an airport diagram or taxi chart during taxi, if published, and maintain situational awareness. ^pa-ii-d-s2

- **PA.II.D.S3** Position the flight controls for the existing wind, if applicable. ^pa-ii-d-s3

- **PA.II.D.S4** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-ii-d-s4

- **PA.II.D.S5** Perform a brake check immediately after the airplane begins moving. ^pa-ii-d-s5

- **PA.II.D.S6** Maintain positive control of the airplane during ground operations by controlling direction and speed without excessive use of brakes. ^pa-ii-d-s6

- **PA.II.D.S7** Comply with airport/taxiway markings, signals, and air traffic control (ATC) clearances and instructions. ^pa-ii-d-s7

- **PA.II.D.S8** Position the airplane properly relative to hold lines. ^pa-ii-d-s8
