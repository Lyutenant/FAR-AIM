# PA.VI.C — Diversion

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=47)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.VI|Area of Operation VI — Navigation]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; VFR Navigation Charts

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with diversion.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.VI.C.K1** Selecting an alternate destination. ^pa-vi-c-k1

- **PA.VI.C.K2** Situations that require deviations from flight plan or air traffic control (ATC) instructions. ^pa-vi-c-k2

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.VI.C.R1** Collision hazards. ^pa-vi-c-r1

- **PA.VI.C.R2** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-vi-c-r2

- **PA.VI.C.R3** Circumstances that would make diversion prudent. ^pa-vi-c-r3

- **PA.VI.C.R4** Selecting an appropriate airport or seaplane base. ^pa-vi-c-r4

- **PA.VI.C.R5** Using available resources (e.g., automation, ATC, and flight deck planning aids). ^pa-vi-c-r5

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.VI.C.S1** Select a suitable destination and route for diversion. ^pa-vi-c-s1

- **PA.VI.C.S2** Make a reasonable estimate of heading, groundspeed, arrival time, and fuel required to the “divert to” destination. ^pa-vi-c-s2

- **PA.VI.C.S3** Maintain the selected altitude, ±200 feet and heading, ±15°. ^pa-vi-c-s3

- **PA.VI.C.S4** Update/interpret weather in flight. ^pa-vi-c-s4

- **PA.VI.C.S5** Use displays of digital weather and aeronautical information, as applicable to maintain situational awareness. ^pa-vi-c-s5

- **PA.VI.C.S6** Promptly divert toward the destination. ^pa-vi-c-s6
