# PA.V.A — Steep Turns

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=44)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.V|Area of Operation V — Performance Maneuvers and Ground Reference Maneuvers]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with steep turns.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.V.A.K1** How to conduct a proper steep turn. ^pa-v-a-k1

- **PA.V.A.K2** Aerodynamics associated with steep turns, including: ^pa-v-a-k2

    - **PA.V.A.K2a** a. Maintaining coordinated flight ^pa-v-a-k2a

    - **PA.V.A.K2b** b. Overbanking tendencies ^pa-v-a-k2b

    - **PA.V.A.K2c** c. Maneuvering speed, including the impact of weight changes ^pa-v-a-k2c

    - **PA.V.A.K2d** d. Load factor and accelerated stalls ^pa-v-a-k2d

    - **PA.V.A.K2e** e. Rate and radius of turn ^pa-v-a-k2e

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.V.A.R1** Division of attention between aircraft control and orientation. ^pa-v-a-r1

- **PA.V.A.R2** Collision hazards. ^pa-v-a-r2

- **PA.V.A.R3** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-v-a-r3

- **PA.V.A.R4** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-v-a-r4

- **PA.V.A.R5** Uncoordinated flight. ^pa-v-a-r5

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.V.A.S1** Clear the area. ^pa-v-a-s1

- **PA.V.A.S2** Establish the manufacturer's recommended airspeed; or if one is not available, an airspeed not to exceed the maneuvering speed (VA). ^pa-v-a-s2

- **PA.V.A.S3** Roll into a coordinated 360° steep turn with approximately a 45° bank. ^pa-v-a-s3

- **PA.V.A.S4** Perform the Task in the opposite direction, as specified by evaluator. ^pa-v-a-s4

- **PA.V.A.S5** Maintain the entry altitude ±100 feet, airspeed ±10 knots, bank ±5°, and roll out on the entry heading ±10°. ^pa-v-a-s5
