# PA.IV.K — Rough Water Takeoff and Climb (ASES, AMES)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=39)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IV|Area of Operation IV — Takeoffs, Landings, and Go-Arounds]] · **Applies to:** ASES, AMES

## References

AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-23, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

- [[AIM]]

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with rough water takeoff and climb.

> [!note] Note
> If a rough water condition does not exist, the applicant must be evaluated by simulating the Task.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IV.K.K1** Effects of atmospheric conditions, including wind, on takeoff and climb performance. ^pa-iv-k-k1

- **PA.IV.K.K2** Best angle of climb speed (VX) and best rate of climb speed (VY). ^pa-iv-k-k2

- **PA.IV.K.K3** Appropriate airplane configuration. ^pa-iv-k-k3

- **PA.IV.K.K4** Appropriate use of rough water takeoff and climb technique. ^pa-iv-k-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IV.K.R1** Selection of takeoff path based on pilot capability, airplane performance and limitations, available distance, and wind. ^pa-iv-k-r1

- **PA.IV.K.R2** Effects of: ^pa-iv-k-r2

    - **PA.IV.K.R2a** a. Crosswind ^pa-iv-k-r2a

    - **PA.IV.K.R2b** b. Windshear ^pa-iv-k-r2b

    - **PA.IV.K.R2c** c. Tailwind ^pa-iv-k-r2c

    - **PA.IV.K.R2d** d. Wake turbulence ^pa-iv-k-r2d

    - **PA.IV.K.R2e** e. Water surface/condition ^pa-iv-k-r2e

- **PA.IV.K.R3** Abnormal operations, including planning for: ^pa-iv-k-r3

    - **PA.IV.K.R3a** a. Rejected takeoff ^pa-iv-k-r3a

    - **PA.IV.K.R3b** b. Potential engine failure in takeoff/climb phase of flight ^pa-iv-k-r3b

- **PA.IV.K.R4** Collision hazards. ^pa-iv-k-r4

- **PA.IV.K.R5** Low altitude maneuvering, including stall, spin, or controlled flight into terrain (CFIT). ^pa-iv-k-r5

- **PA.IV.K.R6** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iv-k-r6

- **PA.IV.K.R7** Gear position in an amphibious airplane. ^pa-iv-k-r7

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IV.K.S1** Complete the appropriate checklist(s). ^pa-iv-k-s1

- **PA.IV.K.S2** Make radio calls as appropriate. ^pa-iv-k-s2

- **PA.IV.K.S3** Verify assigned/correct takeoff path. ^pa-iv-k-s3

- **PA.IV.K.S4** Determine wind direction with or without visible wind direction indicators. ^pa-iv-k-s4

- **PA.IV.K.S5** Position flight controls and configure the airplane for the existing conditions. ^pa-iv-k-s5

- **PA.IV.K.S6** Clear the area, select an appropriate takeoff path considering wind, swells, surface hazards, or vessels. ^pa-iv-k-s6

    - **PA.IV.K.S6a** a. Retract the water rudders, as appropriate ^pa-iv-k-s6a

    - **PA.IV.K.S6b** b. Advance the throttle smoothly to takeoff power and confirm proper engine and flight instrument indications prior to rotation ^pa-iv-k-s6b

- **PA.IV.K.S7** \[Archived] ^pa-iv-k-s7

- **PA.IV.K.S8** Establish and maintain an appropriate planing attitude, directional control, and correct for porpoising, skipping, and increase in water drag. ^pa-iv-k-s8

- **PA.IV.K.S9** Avoid excessive water spray on the propeller(s). ^pa-iv-k-s9

- **PA.IV.K.S10** Lift off at minimum airspeed and accelerate to VY +10/- 5 knots before leaving ground effect. ^pa-iv-k-s10

- **PA.IV.K.S11** Configure the airplane after a positive rate of climb has been verified or in accordance with airplane manufacturer’s instructions. ^pa-iv-k-s11

- **PA.IV.K.S12** Maintain VY +10/-5 knots to a safe maneuvering altitude. ^pa-iv-k-s12

- **PA.IV.K.S13** Maintain directional control and proper wind-drift correction throughout takeoff and climb. ^pa-iv-k-s13
