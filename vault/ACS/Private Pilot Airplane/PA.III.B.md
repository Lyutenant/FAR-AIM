# PA.III.B — Traffic Patterns

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=24)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.III|Area of Operation III — Airport and Seaplane Base Operations]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with traffic patterns.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.III.B.K1** Towered and nontowered airport operations. ^pa-iii-b-k1

- **PA.III.B.K2** Traffic pattern selection for the current conditions. ^pa-iii-b-k2

- **PA.III.B.K3** Right-of-way rules. ^pa-iii-b-k3

- **PA.III.B.K4** Use of automated weather and airport information. ^pa-iii-b-k4

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.III.B.R1** Collision hazards. ^pa-iii-b-r1

- **PA.III.B.R2** Distractions, task prioritization, loss of situational awareness, or disorientation. ^pa-iii-b-r2

- **PA.III.B.R3** Windshear and wake turbulence. ^pa-iii-b-r3

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.III.B.S1** Identify and interpret airport/seaplane base runways, taxiways, markings, signs, and lighting. ^pa-iii-b-s1

- **PA.III.B.S2** Comply with recommended traffic pattern procedures. ^pa-iii-b-s2

- **PA.III.B.S3** Correct for wind drift to maintain the proper ground track. ^pa-iii-b-s3

- **PA.III.B.S4** Maintain orientation with the runway/landing area in use. ^pa-iii-b-s4

- **PA.III.B.S5** Maintain traffic pattern altitude, ±100 feet, and the appropriate airspeed, ±10 knots. ^pa-iii-b-s5

- **PA.III.B.S6** Maintain situational awareness and proper spacing from other aircraft in the traffic pattern. ^pa-iii-b-s6
