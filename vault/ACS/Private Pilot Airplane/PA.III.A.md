# PA.III.A — Communications, Light Signals, and Runway Lighting Systems

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=24)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.III|Area of Operation III — Airport and Seaplane Base Operations]] · **Applies to:** all classes

14 CFR part 91; AIM; FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with normal and emergency radio communications, air traffic control (ATC) light signals, and runway lighting systems.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.III.A.K1** How to obtain appropriate radio frequencies. ^pa-iii-a-k1

- **PA.III.A.K2** Proper radio communication procedures and air traffic control (ATC) phraseology. ^pa-iii-a-k2

- **PA.III.A.K3** ATC light signal recognition. ^pa-iii-a-k3

- **PA.III.A.K4** Appropriate use of transponder(s). ^pa-iii-a-k4

- **PA.III.A.K5** Lost communication procedures. ^pa-iii-a-k5

- **PA.III.A.K6** Equipment issues that could cause loss of communication. ^pa-iii-a-k6

- **PA.III.A.K7** Radar assistance. ^pa-iii-a-k7

- **PA.III.A.K8** National Transportation Safety Board (NTSB) accident/incident reporting. ^pa-iii-a-k8

- **PA.III.A.K9** Runway Status Lighting Systems. ^pa-iii-a-k9

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.III.A.R1** Communication. ^pa-iii-a-r1

- **PA.III.A.R2** Deciding if and when to declare an emergency. ^pa-iii-a-r2

- **PA.III.A.R3** \[Archived] ^pa-iii-a-r3

- **PA.III.A.R4** Use of non-standard phraseology. ^pa-iii-a-r4

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.III.A.S1** Select and activate appropriate frequencies. ^pa-iii-a-s1

- **PA.III.A.S2** Transmit using standard phraseology and procedures as specified in the Aeronautical Information Manual (AIM) and Pilot/Controller Glossary. ^pa-iii-a-s2

- **PA.III.A.S3** Acknowledge radio communications and comply with ATC instructions or as directed by the evaluator. ^pa-iii-a-s3
