# PA.IX.D — Emergency Equipment and Survival Gear

> [!info] Source
> FAA Airman Certification Standards, Private Pilot for Airplane Category (FAA-S-ACS-6C), November 2023, effective 2024-05-31 — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/training_testing/testing/acs/private_airplane_acs_6.pdf#page=61)

**Area of Operation:** [[PA.IX|Area of Operation IX — Emergency Operations]] · **Applies to:** all classes

## References

FAA-H-8083-2, FAA-H-8083-3, FAA-H-8083-25; POH/AFM

## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with emergency equipment, and survival gear appropriate to the airplane and environment encountered during flight.

> [!note] Note
> See Appendix 3: Aircraft, Equipment, and Operational Requirements & Limitations for information related to this Task.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.IX.D.K1** Emergency Locator Transmitter (ELT) operations, limitations, and testing requirements. ^pa-ix-d-k1

- **PA.IX.D.K2** Fire extinguisher operations and limitations. ^pa-ix-d-k2

- **PA.IX.D.K3** Emergency equipment and survival gear needed for: ^pa-ix-d-k3

    - **PA.IX.D.K3a** a. Climate extremes (hot/cold) ^pa-ix-d-k3a

    - **PA.IX.D.K3b** b. Mountainous terrain ^pa-ix-d-k3b

    - **PA.IX.D.K3c** c. Overwater operations ^pa-ix-d-k3c

- **PA.IX.D.K4** When to deploy a ballistic parachute and associated passenger briefings, if equipped. ^pa-ix-d-k4

- **PA.IX.D.K5** When to activate an emergency auto-land system and brief passengers, if equipped. ^pa-ix-d-k5

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.IX.D.R1** Survival gear (water, clothing, shelter) for 48 to 72 hours. ^pa-ix-d-r1

- **PA.IX.D.R2** Use of a ballistic parachute system. ^pa-ix-d-r2

- **PA.IX.D.R3** Use of an emergency auto-land system, if installed. ^pa-ix-d-r3

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.IX.D.S1** Identify appropriate equipment and personal gear. ^pa-ix-d-s1

- **PA.IX.D.S2** Brief passengers on proper use of on-board emergency equipment and survival gear. ^pa-ix-d-s2

- **PA.IX.D.S3** Simulate ballistic parachute deployment procedures, if equipped. ^pa-ix-d-s3
