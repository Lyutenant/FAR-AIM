## Objective

To determine the applicant exhibits satisfactory knowledge, risk management, and skills associated with airman and medical certificates including privileges, limitations, currency, and operating as pilot-in-command as a private pilot.

## Knowledge

The applicant demonstrates understanding of:

- **PA.I.A.K1** Certification requirements, recent flight experience, and recordkeeping. ^pa-i-a-k1

- **PA.I.A.K2** Privileges and limitations. ^pa-i-a-k2

- **PA.I.A.K3** Medical certificates: class, expiration, privileges, temporary disqualifications. ^pa-i-a-k3

- **PA.I.A.K4** Documents required to exercise private pilot privileges. ^pa-i-a-k4

- **PA.I.A.K5** Part 68 BasicMed privileges and limitations. ^pa-i-a-k5

## Risk Management

The applicant is able to identify, assess, and mitigate risk associated with:

- **PA.I.A.R1** Proficiency versus currency. ^pa-i-a-r1

- **PA.I.A.R2** Flying unfamiliar aircraft or operating with unfamiliar flight display systems and avionics. ^pa-i-a-r2

## Skills

The applicant exhibits the skill to:

- **PA.I.A.S1** Apply requirements to act as pilot-in-command (PIC) under Visual Flight Rules (VFR) in a scenario given by the evaluator. ^pa-i-a-s1
