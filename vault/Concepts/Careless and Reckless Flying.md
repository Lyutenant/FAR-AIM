# Careless and Reckless Flying

> [!note] Curated concept
> A study aid from the curated concept graph (`data/enrichment/concepts.json`), not official text. Area: Foundations.

Independent of every specific rule, no one may operate an aircraft carelessly or recklessly, interfere with a crewmember, or drop objects that endanger people or property.
