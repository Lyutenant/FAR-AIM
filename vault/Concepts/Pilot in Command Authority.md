# Pilot in Command Authority

> [!note] Curated concept
> A study aid from the curated concept graph (`data/enrichment/concepts.json`), not official text. Area: Foundations.

The pilot in command is directly responsible for, and the final authority over, the operation of the aircraft, and may deviate from the rules to the extent an in-flight emergency requires.
