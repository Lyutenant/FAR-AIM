# WAKE TURBULENCE

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-w.html#WAKE_TURBULENCE)

## Official Text

WAKE TURBULENCE- A phenomenon that occurs when an aircraft develops lift and forms a pair of counter‐rotating vortices.
