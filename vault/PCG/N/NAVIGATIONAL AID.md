# NAVIGATIONAL AID

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-n.html#NAVIGATIONAL_AID)

## Official Text

NAVIGATIONAL AID- Any visual or electronic device airborne or on the surface which provides point‐to‐point guidance information or position data to aircraft in flight.
