# TRANSITIONAL AIRSPACE

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-t.html#TRANSITIONAL_AIRSPACE)

## Official Text

TRANSITIONAL AIRSPACE- That portion of controlled airspace wherein aircraft change from one phase of flight or flight condition to another.
