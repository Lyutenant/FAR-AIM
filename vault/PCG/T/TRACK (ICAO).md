# TRACK \[ICAO]

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-t.html#TRACK)

## Official Text

TRACK \[ICAO]- The projection on the earth's surface of the path of an aircraft, the direction of which path at any point is usually expressed in degrees from North (True, Magnetic, or Grid).
