# INTERSECTION

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-i.html#INTERSECTION)

## Official Text

INTERSECTION-

- **a.** A point defined by any combination of courses, radials, or bearings of two or more navigational aids.

- **b.** Used to describe the point where two runways, a runway and a taxiway, or two taxiways cross or meet.
