# INCERFA (Uncertainty Phase) \[ICAO]

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-i.html)

## Official Text

INCERFA (Uncertainty Phase) \[ICAO]- A situation wherein uncertainty exists as to the safety of an aircraft and its occupants.
