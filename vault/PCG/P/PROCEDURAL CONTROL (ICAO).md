# PROCEDURAL CONTROL \[ICAO]

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-p.html)

## Official Text

PROCEDURAL CONTROL \[ICAO]– Term used to indicate that information derived from an ATS surveillance system is not required for the provision of air traffic control service.
