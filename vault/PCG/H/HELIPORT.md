# HELIPORT

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-h.html#HELIPORT)

## Official Text

HELIPORT- An area of land, water, or structure used or intended to be used for the landing and takeoff of helicopters and includes its buildings and facilities if any.
