# AREA NAVIGATION (RNAV)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-a.html#AREA_NAVIGATION_RNAV)

## Official Text

AREA NAVIGATION (RNAV)- A method of navigation which permits aircraft operation on any desired flight path within the coverage of ground- or space-based navigation aids or within the limits of the capability of self‐contained aids, or a combination of these.

> [!note] Note:
> Area navigation includes performance-based navigation as well as other operations that do not meet the definition of performance-based navigation.
