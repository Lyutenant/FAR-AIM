# VERTICAL TAKEOFF AND LANDING AIRCRAFT (VTOL)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-v.html#VERTICAL_TAKEOFF_AND_LANDING_AIRCRAFT_VTOL)

## Official Text

VERTICAL TAKEOFF AND LANDING AIRCRAFT (VTOL)- Aircraft capable of vertical climbs and/or descents and of using very short runways or small areas for takeoff and landings. These aircraft include, but are not limited to, helicopters.
