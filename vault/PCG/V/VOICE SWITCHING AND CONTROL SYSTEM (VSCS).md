# VOICE SWITCHING AND CONTROL SYSTEM (VSCS)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-v.html#VOICE_SWITCHING_AND_CONTROL_SYSTEM_VSCS)

## Official Text

VOICE SWITCHING AND CONTROL SYSTEM (VSCS)- A computer controlled switching system that provides air traffic controllers with all voice circuits (air to ground and ground to ground) necessary for air traffic control.
