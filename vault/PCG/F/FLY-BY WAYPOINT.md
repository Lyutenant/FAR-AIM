# FLY‐BY WAYPOINT

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-f.html)

## Official Text

FLY‐BY WAYPOINT- A fly‐by waypoint requires the use of turn anticipation to avoid overshoot of the next flight segment.
