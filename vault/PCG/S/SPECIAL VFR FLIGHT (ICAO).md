# SPECIAL VFR FLIGHT \[ICAO]

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-s.html)

## Official Text

SPECIAL VFR FLIGHT \[ICAO]- A VFR flight cleared by air traffic control to operate within Class B, C, D, and E surface areas in meteorological conditions below VMC.
