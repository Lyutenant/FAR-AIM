# STRAIGHT‐IN LANDING

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-s.html)

## Official Text

STRAIGHT‐IN LANDING- A landing made on a runway aligned within 30° of the final approach course following completion of an instrument approach.
