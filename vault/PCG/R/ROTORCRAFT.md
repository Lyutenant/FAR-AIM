# ROTORCRAFT

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-r.html#ROTORCRAFT)

## Official Text

ROTORCRAFT- A heavier‐than‐air aircraft that depends principally for its support in flight on the lift generated by one or more rotors.
