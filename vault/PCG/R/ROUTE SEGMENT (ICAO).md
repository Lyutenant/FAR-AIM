# ROUTE SEGMENT \[ICAO]

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-r.html#ROUTE_SEGMENT)

## Official Text

ROUTE SEGMENT \[ICAO]- A portion of a route to be flown, as defined by two consecutive significant points specified in a flight plan.
