# COMBINED CONTROL FACILITY (CCF)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-c.html#COMBINED_CONTROL_FACILITY_CCF)

## Official Text

COMBINED CONTROL FACILITY (CCF)- An air traffic facility that combines the functions of an ARTCC and a radar approach control facility.
