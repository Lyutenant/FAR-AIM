# CRUISE CLIMB

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-c.html#CRUISE_CLIMB)

## Official Text

CRUISE CLIMB- A climb technique employed by aircraft, usually at a constant power setting, resulting in an increase of altitude as the aircraft weight decreases.
