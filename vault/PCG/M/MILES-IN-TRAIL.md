# MILES‐IN‐TRAIL

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-m.html)

## Official Text

MILES‐IN‐TRAIL- A specified distance between aircraft, normally, in the same stratum associated with the same destination or route of flight.
