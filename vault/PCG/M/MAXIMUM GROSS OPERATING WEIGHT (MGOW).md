# MAXIMUM GROSS OPERATING WEIGHT (MGOW)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-m.html#MAXIMUM_GROSS_OPERATING_WEIGHT_MGOW)

## Official Text

MAXIMUM GROSS OPERATING WEIGHT (MGOW)- The maximum gross weight of an aircraft, including fuel and any external objects, at any point during the flight.
