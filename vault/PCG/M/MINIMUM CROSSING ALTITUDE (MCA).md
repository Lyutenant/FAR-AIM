# MINIMUM CROSSING ALTITUDE (MCA)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Pilot/Controller Glossary, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/pcg_html/glossary-m.html#MINIMUM_CROSSING_ALTITUDE_MCA)

## Official Text

MINIMUM CROSSING ALTITUDE (MCA)- The lowest altitude at certain fixes at which an aircraft must cross when proceeding in the direction of a higher minimum en route IFR altitude (MEA).
