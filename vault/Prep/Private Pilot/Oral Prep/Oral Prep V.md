**Scenario 1.** What do you do before every steep turn and why?
**Answer (study aid):** Clearing turns, looking below and behind the wings, verbalizing clear left and right, and choosing an altitude with recovery room; the AIM asks for clearing procedures before all turns and maneuvers.

**Scenario 1.** You are doing turns around a point at 800 ft AGL over a farm. Is that legal, and what should you check first?
**Answer (study aid):** Legal over sparsely populated areas if you stay 500 ft from any person, vessel, vehicle or structure and can land without undue hazard if the engine fails; over a congested area you need 1,000 ft above the highest obstacle within 2,000 ft. Check for towers, wires and MTRs on the chart.
**Cites:** [[91.119|§ 91.119 — Minimum safe altitudes: General]], [[3-5-2|AIM 3-5-2 — Military Training Routes]]
