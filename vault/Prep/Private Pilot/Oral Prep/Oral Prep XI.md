**Scenario 1.** What do you need to be night current for passengers and what counts as night for that?
**Answer (study aid):** Three takeoffs and landings to a full stop in the preceding 90 days during the period 1 hour after sunset to 1 hour before sunrise, in the same category, class and type.
**Find it:** § 61.57(b); note the three different definitions of night (§ 1.1, § 91.209, § 61.57).
**Cites:** [[61.57|§ 61.57 — Recent flight experience: Pilot in command]]

**Scenario 2.** Which lights must be on at night and from when?
**Answer (study aid):** Position lights from sunset to sunrise, anticollision lights whenever operating unless the PIC decides they are a hazard; the AIM adds beacon before start, all exterior lights on the runway, landing light with the takeoff clearance.
**Cites:** [[91.209|§ 91.209 — Aircraft lights]], [[4-3-24|AIM 4-3-24 — Use of Aircraft Lights]]

**Scenario 3.** How do you tell another aircraft's direction of flight from its lights?
**Answer (study aid):** Red on the left wing, green on the right, white on the tail: red on my right and green on my left means it is coming toward me; a white light alone is going away.
**Cites:** [[91.209|§ 91.209 — Aircraft lights]], [[4-3-24|AIM 4-3-24 — Use of Aircraft Lights]]

**Scenario 4.** You are landing on a narrow runway with no VASI over dark terrain. Which illusions and what do you do?
**Answer (study aid):** A narrow runway and featureless terrain both make you feel high and fly a low approach; use the normal aiming point and pattern altitudes, a VASI or PAPI if any, and fly the known pitch and power.
**Cites:** [[8-1-5|AIM 8-1-5 — Illusions in Flight]]

**Scenario 5.** How do you turn on the runway lights at a non-towered airport, and how do you tell the airport from the beacon?
**Answer (study aid):** Key the mic 7 times on the CTAF for high intensity (5 medium, 3 low), 15 minutes at a time; a white and green beacon is a lighted land airport, white and yellow is water, and a military field has two white flashes between greens.
**Cites:** [[2-1-8|AIM 2-1-8 — Pilot Control of Airport Lighting]], [[2-1-9|AIM 2-1-9 — Airport/Heliport Beacons]]

**Scenario 6.** How long does dark adaptation take and what defeats it?
**Answer (study aid):** About 30 minutes in darkness (20 under dim red light); bright white light, including your own strobes reflecting off cloud, and hypoxia above 5,000 ft degrade it. Keep interior lights low.
**Cites:** [[8-1-6|AIM 8-1-6 — Vision in Flight]], [[8-1-2|AIM 8-1-2 — Effects of Altitude]]
