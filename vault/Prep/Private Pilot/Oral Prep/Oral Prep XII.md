**Scenario 1.** After landing you notice the left brake was weak. What do you do with that?
**Answer (study aid):** Write it up: tell the operator and record the discrepancy so maintenance can address it; § 91.405 requires discrepancies be repaired between inspections and the next pilot must know. Then secure the airplane: mixture, magnetos and master off, controls locked, chocks and tie-downs.
**Cites:** [[91.405|§ 91.405 — Maintenance required]], [[91.417|§ 91.417 — Maintenance records]]

**Scenario 2.** Passengers want to get out while the propeller winds down. Answer?
**Answer (study aid):** No one leaves until the engine is stopped and the beacon is off; I brief the ramp route away from other propellers and jet blast and escort them.
**Cites:** [[4-3-24|AIM 4-3-24 — Use of Aircraft Lights]], [[91.13|§ 91.13 — Careless or reckless operation]]
