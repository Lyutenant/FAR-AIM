# AIM 6-5-2 — Radio Call Signs

> [!info] Source
> FAA Aeronautical Information Manual, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap6_section_5.html#6-5-2)

## Official Text

Preferred radio call sign for the ARFF IC is “(location/facility) Command” when communicating with the flight crew and the FAA ATCT.

> [!example] EXAMPLE-
> LAX Command.\
> Washington Command.
