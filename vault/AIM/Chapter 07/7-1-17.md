# AIM 7-1-17 — Estimating Intensity of Snow or Drizzle (Based on Visibility)

> [!info] Source
> FAA Aeronautical Information Manual, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap7_section_1.html#7-1-17)

## Official Text

**a.** Light. Visibility more than 1/2 statute mile.

**b.** Moderate. Visibility from more than 1/4 statute mile to 1/2 statute mile.

**c.** Heavy. Visibility 1/4 statute mile or less.
