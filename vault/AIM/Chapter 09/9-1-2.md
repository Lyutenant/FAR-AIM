# AIM 9-1-2 — Obtaining Aeronautical Charts

## Official Text

Public sales of charts and publications are available through a network of FAA approved print providers. A listing of products, dates of latest editions and agents is available on the AIS website at: https://www.faa.gov/air\_traffic/flight\_info/aeronav/safety\_alerts/.
