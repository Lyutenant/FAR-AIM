# AIM 4-1-1 — Air Route Traffic Control Centers

> [!info] Source
> FAA Aeronautical Information Manual, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap4_section_1.html#4-1-1)

> [!study] Private Pilot study aid — curated, not official text
> **Gist:** Air Route Traffic Control Centers exist primarily for IFR aircraft in controlled airspace during the en route phase; VFR flight following from Center is a workload-permitting extra.
> **Why it matters:** It explains who you are talking to when approach hands you off to 'Center' on a long cross-country.
> **Numbers:** Center serves IFR en route
> **ACS:** [[PA.VIII.F#^pa-viii-f-k3|PA.VIII.F.K3]] · [[PA.I.D#^pa-i-d-r5|PA.I.D.R5]] — **Stage:** solo-xc

## Official Text

Centers are established primarily to provide air traffic service to aircraft operating on IFR flight plans within controlled airspace, and principally during the en route phase of flight.
