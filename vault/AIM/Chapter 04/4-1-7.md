# AIM 4-1-7 — Operation Rain Check

> [!info] Source
> FAA Aeronautical Information Manual, Basic with Change 1, 2 and 3 (effective 2026-07-09) — [view on FAA](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/aim_html/chap4_section_1.html#4-1-7)

## Official Text

Operation Rain Check is a program designed and managed by local air traffic control facility management. Its purpose is to familiarize pilots and aspiring pilots with the ATC system, its functions, responsibilities and benefits.
