# Currency and Flight Review

Part of [[Private Pilot]]. Being certificated is not the same as being
current — these rules decide whether you may act as pilot in command,
and whether you may carry passengers, today.

## Governing regulations

- [[61.56|§ 61.56 — Flight review]]
- [[61.57|§ 61.57 — Recent flight experience: Pilot in command]] — passenger-carrying
  currency, day and night
- [[61.23|§ 61.23 — Medical certificates: Requirement and duration]]
- [[1.1|§ 1.1 — General definitions]] — the FAR definition of *night*
  (civil twilight), which differs from the night-currency window in
  § 61.57(b) and from the position-light rule in
  [[91.209|§ 91.209 — Aircraft lights]]
