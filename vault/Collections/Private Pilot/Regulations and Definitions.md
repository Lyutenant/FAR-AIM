# Regulations and Definitions

Part of [[Private Pilot]]. How Title 14 is organized, which parts a
student or private pilot actually lives in, and where the words the
rules depend on are defined. Start here before any other page.

## How the rules are organized

- [[Title 14|Title 14, Code of Federal Regulations]] — the whole title;
  the parts below are the ones a private pilot works with
- [[Part 1|Part 1 — Definitions and Abbreviations]] — the vocabulary every
  other part uses
- [[Part 61|Part 61 — Certification: Pilots, Flight Instructors, and Ground Instructors]]
- [[Part 67|Part 67 — Medical Standards and Certification]] · [[Part 68|Part 68 — BasicMed]]
- [[Part 91|Part 91 — General Operating and Flight Rules]]
- [[Part 141|Part 141 — Pilot Schools]] — training under an approved
  course; the certificate you earn is the same Part 61 certificate
- [[Part 43|Part 43 — Maintenance]] · [[Part 39|Part 39 — Airworthiness Directives]]
- [[Part 71|Part 71 — Designation of airspace]] · [[Part 73|Part 73 — Special Use Airspace]] ·
  [[Part 99|Part 99 — Security Control of Air Traffic]]
- [[AIM|Aeronautical Information Manual]] — procedures and good practice,
  not regulation; the [[AIM 0-0|Explanation of Changes]] page tracks each edition

- [[1.2|§ 1.2 — Abbreviations and symbols]]
- [[1.3|§ 1.3 — Rules of construction]] — how to read "shall", "may",
