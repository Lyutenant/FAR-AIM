# Private Pilot

A curated map of the regulations, AIM guidance, and glossary terms a
private pilot (airplane, single-engine) works with most. Every page
links to the generated authoritative notes — the official text lives
there, and rebuilds never touch this folder.

For the order in which these subjects build on one another, start at the
[[Concept Map]].

## The rules and the paperwork

- [[Regulations and Definitions]] — how Title 14 is organized, Part 1 definitions, Part 61 vs 141
- [[Operating Rules and Conduct]] — PIC authority, careless operation, alcohol, passengers, aerobatics
- [[Logbooks and Documents]] — what you carry and log; what the aircraft carries

## Certificates and training

- [[Pilot Qualifications]] — eligibility, knowledge, proficiency, experience, privileges, tests
- [[Medical and BasicMed]] — medical certificates, BasicMed, aeromedical factors
- [[Student Pilot and Solo]] — solo and solo cross-country requirements
- [[Currency and Flight Review]] — flight review, recent experience, night currency

## The aircraft

- [[Airworthiness]] — whose job it is that the aircraft may fly; inspections
- [[Required Equipment]] — instruments, lights, transponders, ADS-B, inoperative equipment

## Airports and airspace

- [[Airport Operations and Traffic Patterns]] — markings, signs, lighting, taxiing, the pattern
- [[Airspace Classes]] — classes, special use airspace, TFRs, national security airspace
- [[Right of Way]] — see-and-avoid, right-of-way, speed, minimum altitudes
- [[ATC Operations]] — phraseology, clearances, towered and non-towered airports, radar services

## Weather and planning

- [[Weather Products and Hazards]] — briefings, METAR/TAF, advisories, thunderstorms, icing, wind shear
- [[VFR Weather Minimums]] — visibility, cloud clearance, special VFR
- [[Preflight Requirements]] — § 91.103, briefings, NOTAMs, flight plans, diversions
- [[Navigation and Charts]] — VOR, GPS, NDB, charts and the Chart Supplement, cruising altitudes
- [[Night Flying]] — the three "nights", night equipment, lighting, night vision

## When things go wrong

- [[Hazards and Wake Turbulence]] — wake vortices, wildlife, obstructions, drones, lasers
- [[Emergencies]] — PIC authority, declaring, transponder codes, lost comms
- [[NTSB Reporting]] — accident and incident notification
