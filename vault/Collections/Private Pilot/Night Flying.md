# Night Flying

Part of [[Private Pilot]]. Three different "nights" in the rules,
the equipment and currency night demands, and the lighting and
physiology that make it different.

## Governing regulations

- [[1.1|§ 1.1 — General definitions]] — *night* is the end of evening
  civil twilight to the beginning of morning civil twilight
- [[91.209|§ 91.209 — Aircraft lights]] — position lights from sunset to
  sunrise (a different window from the definition)
- [[61.57|§ 61.57 — Recent flight experience: Pilot in command]] — night
  passenger currency counts landings between one hour after sunset and
  one hour before sunrise (a third window)
- [[91.205|§ 91.205 — Instrument and equipment requirements]] — the
  night VFR list in paragraph (c)
- [[61.109|§ 61.109 — Aeronautical experience]] — the 3 hours of night
  training, the night cross-country and the 10 night landings
- [[61.87|§ 61.87 — Solo requirements for student pilots]] — the
  additional training and endorsement for solo at night
- [[61.110|§ 61.110 — Night flying exceptions]]
- [[91.155|§ 91.155 — Basic VFR weather minimums]] — night raises the
  Class G minimums
