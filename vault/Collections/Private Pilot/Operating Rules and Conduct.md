# Operating Rules and Conduct

Part of [[Private Pilot]]. The Part 91 rules that apply to every flight
regardless of airspace or weather: who is in charge, what conduct is
prohibited, and what is owed to passengers. The traffic and airspace
rules are on [[Right of Way]] and [[Airspace Classes]].
