# Navigation and Charts

Part of [[Private Pilot]]. The radio aids a VFR pilot uses, the charts
and publications a cross-country is planned from, and the altitude
rules that shape the route. Pilotage, dead reckoning and the wind
triangle are handbook material (PHAK chapter 16) rather than
regulation; only the glossary terms appear here.
