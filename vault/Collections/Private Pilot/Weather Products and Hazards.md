# Weather Products and Hazards

Part of [[Private Pilot]]. Where aviation weather comes from, how to
read the products a briefing is built on, and the hazards the AIM
tells you to recognise and avoid. The legal minimums are on
[[VFR Weather Minimums]]; the briefing duty is on [[Preflight Requirements]].

## Governing regulations

- [[91.103|§ 91.103 — Preflight action]] — weather reports and forecasts
  are part of "all available information"
- [[91.121|§ 91.121 — Altimeter settings]]
