# NTSB Reporting

Part of [[Private Pilot]]. Accident and incident notification is
governed by the NTSB's rules in **49 CFR Part 830** — Title 49, so it is
outside this vault's Title 14 corpus:

- [49 CFR Part 830 on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/part-830) —
  definitions of *accident* and *serious injury*, immediate notification
  (§ 830.5), and report filing (§ 830.15)
