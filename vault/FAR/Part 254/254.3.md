# § 254.3 — Definitions

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-254.3)

## Official Text

*Large aircraft* means any aircraft designed to have a maximum passenger capacity of more than 60 seats.
