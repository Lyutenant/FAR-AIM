# § 91.133 — Restricted and prohibited areas

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-15 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-15/title-14/section-91.133)

> [!study] Private Pilot study aid — curated, not official text
> **Gist:** You may not fly in a restricted area contrary to its restrictions, or in a prohibited area at all, without the permission of the using or controlling agency.
> **Why it matters:** It is the one-line rule behind the sectional's blue-hatched areas; the AIM explains how to find out whether a restricted area is active.
> **Numbers:** Permission comes from the using or controlling agency
> **Watch out:** A restricted area that is not active may be flown through, but only the controlling agency or FSS can tell you it is cold; the chart's published times are a starting point, not permission.
> **ACS:** [[PA.I.E#^pa-i-e-k3|PA.I.E.K3]] — **Stage:** solo-xc

## Official Text

- **(a)** No person may operate an aircraft within a restricted area (designated in part 73) contrary to the restrictions imposed, or within a prohibited area, unless that person has the permission of the using or controlling agency, as appropriate.

- **(b)** Each person conducting, within a restricted area, an aircraft operation (approved by the using agency) that creates the same hazards as the operations for which the restricted area was designated may deviate from the rules of this subpart that are not compatible with the operation of the aircraft.
