# § 47.17 — Fees

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-47.17)

## Official Text

**(a)** The fees for applications under this part are as follows:

<table>
<tbody>
<tr><td>(1) Certificate of Aircraft Registration (each aircraft)</td><td>$5.00</td></tr>
<tr><td>(2) Dealer's Aircraft Registration Certificate</td><td>10.00</td></tr>
<tr><td>(3) Additional Dealer's Aircraft Registration Certificate (issued to same dealer)</td><td>2.00</td></tr>
<tr><td>(4) Special registration number (each number)</td><td>10.00</td></tr>
<tr><td>(5) To change, reassign, or reserve a registration number</td><td>10.00</td></tr>
<tr><td>(6) Replacement Certificate of Aircraft Registration</td><td>2.00</td></tr>
<tr><td>(7) Renewal Certificate of Aircraft Registration</td><td>5.00</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

**(b)** Each application must be accompanied by the proper fee, that may be paid by check or money order to the Federal Aviation Administration.
