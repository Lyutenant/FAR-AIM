# § 295.22 — Prohibited unfair or deceptive practices or unfair methods of competition

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-295.22)
