# § 5.95 — SMS documentation

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-24 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-24/title-14/section-5.95)

## Official Text

Any person required to have an SMS under this part must develop and maintain the following SMS documentation:

**(a)** Safety policy.

**(b)** SMS processes and procedures.
