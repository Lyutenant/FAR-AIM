# § 1201.200 — General

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-1201.200)

## Official Text

NASA's basic organization consists of the Headquarters, nine field Centers, the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (a Federally Funded Research and Development Center), and several component installations which report to Center Directors. Responsibility for overall planning, coordination, and control of NASA programs is vested in NASA Headquarters located in Washington, DC. For additional information, visit http://www.nasa.gov/about/org\_index.html.
