# § 21.51 — Duration

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-24 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-24/title-14/section-21.51)

## Official Text

A type certificate is effective until surrendered, suspended, revoked, or a termination date is otherwise established by the FAA.
