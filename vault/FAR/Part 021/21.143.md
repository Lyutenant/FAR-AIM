# § 21.143 — Duration

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-19 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-19/title-14/section-21.143)

## Official Text

A production certificate is effective until surrendered, suspended, revoked, or the FAA otherwise establishes a termination date.
