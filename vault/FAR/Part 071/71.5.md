# § 71.5 — Reporting points

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-24 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-24/title-14/section-71.5)

## Official Text

The reporting points listed in subpart H of FAA Order JO 7400.11K (incorporated by reference, see § 71.1) consist of geographic locations at which the position of an aircraft must be reported in accordance with part 91 of this chapter.
