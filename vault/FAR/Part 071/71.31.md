# § 71.31 — Class A airspace

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-71.31)

## Official Text

The airspace descriptions contained in § 71.33 and the routes contained in subpart A of FAA Order JO 7400.11K (incorporated by reference, see § 71.1) are designated as Class A airspace within which all pilots and aircraft are subject to the rating requirements, operating rules, and equipment requirements of part 91 of this chapter.
