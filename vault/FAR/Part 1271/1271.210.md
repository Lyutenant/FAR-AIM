# § 1271.210 — Reporting

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-19 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-19/title-14/section-1271.210)

## Official Text

No reporting is required with respect to payments of reasonable compensation made to regularly employed officers or employees of a person.
