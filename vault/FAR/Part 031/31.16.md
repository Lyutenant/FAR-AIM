# § 31.16 — Empty weight

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-31.16)

## Official Text

The empty weight must be determined by weighing the balloon with installed equipment but without lifting gas or heater fuel.
