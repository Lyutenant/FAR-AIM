# § 89.1 — Definitions

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-89.1)

## Official Text

The following definitions apply to this part.

- *Declaration of compliance* means a record submitted to the FAA by the producer of a standard remote identification unmanned aircraft or remote identification broadcast module to attest that all the requirements of subpart F of this part have been met.

- *Home-built unmanned aircraft* means an unmanned aircraft that an individual built solely for education or recreation.
