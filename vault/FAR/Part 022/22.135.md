# § 22.135 — Instruments and equipment

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-19 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-19/title-14/section-22.135)

## Official Text

**(a)** The aircraft must have all instruments and equipment necessary for safe flight, to include those instruments necessary for systems control and management.

**(b)** The aircraft must include all instruments and equipment required for the kinds of operations for which it is authorized.

**(c)** The aircraft's, instruments, equipment, and systems must perform their intended functions under all operating conditions specified in the pilot's operating handbook. Likely failure or malfunction of equipment or a system must not cause loss of control of the aircraft. Equipment and systems must be considered separately and in relation to each other.
