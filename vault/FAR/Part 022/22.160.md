# § 22.160 — Visibility

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-22.160)

## Official Text

The aircraft must be designed and constructed so that the pilot has—

**(a)** Sufficient visibility of controls, instruments, equipment, and placards; and

**(b)** Sufficient visibility outside the aircraft necessary to conduct safe aircraft operations.
