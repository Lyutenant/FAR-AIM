# § 22.150 — Fuel system

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-22.150)

## Official Text

The aircraft fuel system must—

**(a)** Provide a means to safely remove or isolate the fuel stored in the system from the aircraft; and

**(b)** Be designed to retain fuel under all likely operating conditions.
