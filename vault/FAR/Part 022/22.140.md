# § 22.140 — Controls and displays

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-22.140)

## Official Text

The aircraft must be designed and constructed so that the pilot has the ability to reach controls and displays in a manner that provides for smooth and positive operation of the aircraft.
