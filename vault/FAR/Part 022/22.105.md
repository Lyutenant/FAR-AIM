# § 22.105 — Control and maneuverability

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-22.105)

## Official Text

A light-sport category aircraft must—

- **(a)** Be consistently and predictably controllable and maneuverable at all loading conditions during all phases of flight; and,

- **(b)** Not have a tendency to inadvertently depart controlled flight or require exceptional piloting skill, alertness, or strength.
