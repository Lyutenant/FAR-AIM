# § 22.125 — Environmental conditions

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-22.125)

## Official Text

The aircraft must have design characteristics to safely accommodate all environmental conditions likely to be encountered during its intended operations.
