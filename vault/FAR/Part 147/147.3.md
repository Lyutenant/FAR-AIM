# § 147.3 — Certificate required

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-24 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-24/title-14/section-147.3)

## Official Text

No person may operate an aviation maintenance technician school without, or in violation of, an aviation maintenance technician school certificate and the operations specifications issued under this part.
