# § 29.241 — Ground resonance

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-29.241)

## Official Text

The rotorcraft may have no dangerous tendency to oscillate on the ground with the rotor turning.
