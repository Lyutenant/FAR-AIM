# § 29.251 — Vibration

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-24 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-24/title-14/section-29.251)

## Official Text

Each part of the rotorcraft must be free from excessive vibration under each appropriate speed and power condition.
