# § 300.7 — Conciseness

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-24 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-24/title-14/section-300.7)

## Official Text

Every oral or written statement made in a DOT proceding shall be as concise as possible. Verbose or redundant presentations may be rejected.
