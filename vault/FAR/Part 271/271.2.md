# § 271.2 — Definitions

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-271.2)

## Official Text

As used in this part:

- *Eligible place* means a place in the United States that meets the specified criteria outlined in 49 U.S.C. Chapter 417. ^def-eligible-place

- *Essential air service* is that air transportation which the Department has found to be essential under Subchapter II of Chapter 417 of the Statute. ^def-essential-air-service
