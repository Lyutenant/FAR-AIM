# § 1259.302 — Application procedures

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-19 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-19/title-14/section-1259.302)

## Official Text

**(a)** The Administrator or designee has the authority to make awards to meet identified national needs.

**(b)** The Director, NASA Space Grant Program shall establish a competitive, merit-based review process to examine unsolicited national needs proposals.
