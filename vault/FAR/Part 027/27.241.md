# § 27.241 — Ground resonance

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-19 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-19/title-14/section-27.241)

## Official Text

The rotorcraft may have no dangerous tendency to oscillate on the ground with the rotor turning.
