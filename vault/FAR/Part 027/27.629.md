# § 27.629 — Flutter

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-27.629)

## Official Text

Each aerodynamic surface of the rotorcraft must be free from flutter under each appropriate speed and power condition.
