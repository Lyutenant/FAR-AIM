# § 27.1413 — Safety belts

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-27.1413)

## Official Text

Each safety belt must be equipped with a metal to metal latching device.
