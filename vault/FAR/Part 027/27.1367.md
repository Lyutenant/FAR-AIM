# § 27.1367 — Switches

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-27.1367)

## Official Text

Each switch must be—

- **(a)** Able to carry its rated current;

- **(b)** Accessible to the crew; and

- **(c)** Labeled as to operation and the circuit controlled.
