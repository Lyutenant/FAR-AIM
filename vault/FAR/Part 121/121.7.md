# § 121.7 — Definitions

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-121.7)

## Official Text

The following definitions apply to those sections of part 121 that apply to ETOPS:

- *Adequate Airport* means an airport that an airplane operator may list with approval from the FAA because that airport meets the landing limitations of § 121.197 and is either— ^def-adequate-airport

    - **(1)** An airport that meets the requirements of part 139, subpart D of this chapter, excluding those that apply to aircraft rescue and firefighting service, or

    - **(2)** A military airport that is active and operational.

- *ETOPS Alternate Airport* means an adequate airport listed in the certificate holder's operations specifications that is designated in a dispatch or flight release for use in the event of a diversion during ETOPS. This definition applies to flight planning and does not in any way limit the authority of the pilot-in-command during flight. ^def-etops-alternate-airport

- *ETOPS Area of Operation* means one of the following areas: ^def-etops-area-of-operation

    - **(1)** For turbine-engine-powered airplanes with two engines, an area beyond 60 minutes from an adequate airport, computed using a one-engine-inoperative cruise speed under standard conditions in still air.

    - **(2)** For turbine-engine-powered passenger-carrying airplanes with more than two engines, an area beyond 180 minutes from an adequate airport, computed using a one-engine-inoperative cruise speed under standard conditions in still air.

- *ETOPS Entry Point* means the first point on the route of an ETOPS flight, determined using a one-engine-inoperative cruise speed under standard conditions in still air, that is— ^def-etops-entry-point

    - **(1)** More than 60 minutes from an adequate airport for airplanes with two engines;

    - **(2)** More than 180 minutes from an adequate airport for passenger-carrying airplanes with more than two engines.

- *ETOPS Qualified Person* means a person, performing maintenance for the certificate holder, who has satisfactorily completed the certificate holder's ETOPS training program. ^def-etops-qualified-person

- *Maximum Diversion Time* means, for the purposes of ETOPS route planning, the longest diversion time authorized for a flight under the operator's ETOPS authority. It is calculated under standard conditions in still air at a one-engine-inoperative cruise speed. ^def-maximum-diversion-time

- *North Pacific Area of Operation* means Pacific Ocean areas north of 40° N latitudes including NOPAC ATS routes, and published PACOTS tracks between Japan and North America. ^def-north-pacific-area-of-operation

- *North Polar Area* means the entire area north of 78° N latitude. ^def-north-polar-area

- *One-engine-inoperative-Cruise Speed* means a speed within the certified operating limits of the airplane that is specified by the certificate holder and approved by the FAA for — ^def-one-engine-inoperative-cruise-speed

    - **(1)** Calculating required fuel reserves needed to account for an inoperative engine; or

    - **(2)** Determining whether an ETOPS alternate is within the maximum diversion time authorized for an ETOPS flight.

- *South Polar Area* means the entire area South of 60° S latitude. ^def-south-polar-area
