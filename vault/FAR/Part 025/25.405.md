# § 25.405 — Secondary control system

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-08-24 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-08-24/title-14/section-25.405)

## Official Text

Secondary controls, such as wheel brake, spoiler, and tab controls, must be designed for the maximum forces that a pilot is likely to apply to those controls. The following values may be used:

*Pilot Control Force Limits (Secondary Controls)*

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th>Control</th><th>Limit pilot forces</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Miscellaneous:</td><td></td></tr>
<tr><td>*Crank, wheel, or lever</td><td>((1 + R) / 3) × 50 lbs., but not less than 50 lbs. nor more than 150 lbs. (R = radius). (Applicable to any angle within 20° of plane of control).</td></tr>
<tr><td>Twist</td><td>133 in.-lbs.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Push-pull</td><td>To be chosen by applicant.</td></tr>
</tbody>
<tfoot>
<tr><td colspan="2">*Limited to flap, tab, stabilizer, spoiler, and landing gear operation controls.</td></tr>
</tfoot>
</table>
