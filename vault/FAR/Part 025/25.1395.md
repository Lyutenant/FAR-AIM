# § 25.1395 — Maximum intensities in overlapping beams of forward and rear position lights

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-25.1395)

## Official Text

No position light intensity may exceed the applicable values in the following table, except as provided in § 25.1389(b)(3).

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th rowspan="2">Overlaps</th><th colspan="2">Maximum intensity</th></tr>
<tr><th>Area A (candles)</th><th>Area B (candles)</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>Green in dihedral angle L</td><td>10</td><td>1</td></tr>
<tr><td>Red in dihedral angle R</td><td>10</td><td>1</td></tr>
<tr><td>Green in dihedral angle A</td><td>5</td><td>1</td></tr>
<tr><td>Red in dihedral angle A</td><td>5</td><td>1</td></tr>
<tr><td>Rear white in dihedral angle L</td><td>5</td><td>1</td></tr>
<tr><td>Rear white in dihedral angle R</td><td>5</td><td>1</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

Where—

**(a)** Area A includes all directions in the adjacent dihedral angle that pass through the light source and intersect the common boundary plane at more than 10 degrees but less than 20 degrees; and

**(b)** Area B includes all directions in the adjacent dihedral angle that pass through the light source and intersect the common boundary plane at more than 20 degrees.
