# § 25.815 — Width of aisle

> [!info] Source
> eCFR Title 14, issue 2026-09-03 — [view on eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2026-09-03/title-14/section-25.815)

## Official Text

The passenger aisle width at any point between seats must equal or exceed the values in the following table:

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th rowspan="2">Passenger seating capacity</th><th colspan="2">Minimum passenger aisle width (inches)</th></tr>
<tr><th>Less than 25 in. from floor</th><th>25 in. and more from floor</th></tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>10 or less</td><td>1 12</td><td>15</td></tr>
<tr><td>11 through 19</td><td>12</td><td>20</td></tr>
<tr><td>20 or more</td><td>15</td><td>20</td></tr>
</tbody>
<tfoot>
<tr><td colspan="3">1 A narrower width not less than 9 inches may be approved when substantiated by tests found necessary by the Administrator.</td></tr>
</tfoot>
</table>
