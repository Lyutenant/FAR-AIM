# Cross-Country Flight

Where cross-country flight is defined, and the main certificate and
rating requirements built on the definition. The official text lives in
the linked notes — and open [[61.1|§ 61.1]]'s **backlinks** pane for
every note that leans on the definition.

## Where it is defined

[[61.1|§ 61.1(b) — Applicability and definitions]] defines *cross-country
time* in tiers, and the distance that "counts" depends on why you are
logging it:

- **(i)** In general: any flight with a landing at another point using
  navigation — no minimum distance.
- **(ii)** For the aeronautical experience requirements of a private or
  commercial certificate or an instrument rating, and for exercising
  recreational pilot privileges under § 61.101(c) (rotorcraft and powered
  parachute excepted): a landing point **more than 50 NM** straight-line
  from the departure point.
- **(iii)/(iv)** Sport pilot: more than 25 NM; powered parachute: more than 15 NM.
- **(v)** Rotorcraft ratings: more than 25 NM.
- **(vi)** ATP certificate: more than 50 NM straight-line — no landing required.

## Requirements built on it

By certificate or rating, the aeronautical-experience rules that count
cross-country time:

- **Student:** [[61.93|§ 61.93 — Solo cross-country flight requirements]] —
  endorsements; also required before any student flight greater than
  25 NM from the departure airport, or any solo landing anywhere other
  than the departure airport — and [[61.87|§ 61.87 — Solo requirements
  for student pilots]], the prerequisite to any solo flight
- **Sport:** [[61.313|§ 61.313 — What aeronautical experience must I have
  to apply for a sport pilot certificate?]]
- **Recreational:** [[61.99|§ 61.99 — Aeronautical experience]] and
  [[61.101|§ 61.101 — Recreational pilot privileges and limitations]] —
  the distance limits referenced by the definition
- **Private:** [[61.109|§ 61.109 — Aeronautical experience]] — including
  the solo cross-country of 150 NM total with a segment of more than
  50 NM between takeoff and landing
- **Instrument rating:** [[61.65|§ 61.65 — Instrument rating requirements]]
- **Commercial:** [[61.129|§ 61.129 — Aeronautical experience]]
- **ATP:** [[61.159|§ 61.159 — Aeronautical experience: Airplane category rating]]
